# Creative brief (the player's choices — binding on every agent)

- **World:** Old-money Texas dynasty, oil and cattle. Present day (phones, texts, digital footprints).
- **Tone:** Sharp & satirical — mordant wit, awful rich people, comedy that curdles into tragedy. Prestige-TV register.
  Satire targets: rich-people absurdity (galas, jets, horse auctions, prenups), Texas mythmaking (billionaire cowboy cosplay,
  God-and-guns branding), oil & climate PR (greenwashing, "energy transition" theater), politics & media (bought candidates,
  cable news, influencers).
- **Protagonist:** The heir. Man, early 30s, the golden boy — charming, reckless, underestimated by the board.
  Core flaw: charming coward — talks his way around every confrontation until he can't. Has one young child (daughter, ~5).
- **His wife:** Ruthless partner, more ambitious than he is; wants the throne through him and may know more than she lets on.
  She cares less about an affair itself than about it being found out and humiliating them. Her hard line: he never leaves.
- **The patriarch:** Lion in winter — 70s, failing health he hides, still terrifying, testing his children for the succession.
- **Her (the love interest):** The patriarch's new wife, 25. The town and family all have strong opinions (gold-digger).
  Her true nature/secret is HIDDEN from the player — writers decide and keep it hidden; reveal slowly over seasons.
- **The affair:** Both are married (protagonist to his wife; she to his father). Slow-burn romance; consummation not before
  Season 2. The romantic core is protected: darkness everywhere, but the love story can reach a satisfying end if the player
  fights for it.
- **Ensemble:** Rival sibling (better at the business, wants the crown); the family fixer (lawyer or ranch foreman, 30 years of
  cleanups); the matriarch/ex-wife (his mother, discarded first wife, still dangerous, still in the will); outside threats
  (journalist, FBI agent, environmental activist, rival oilman).
- **Storylines:** Succession war (backbone); a buried crime on the ranch from decades ago resurfacing; land & water / mineral
  rights / tribal land with real moral stakes; politics (a Senate or Governor's race being bought or blocked).
- **Arcs:** Mix of durations — single-episode, multi-episode, season-long, multi-season, and full-run arcs. Mysteries, twists,
  reveals, hidden from the player.
- **Run:** 5 seasons × 10 episodes. Each episode ~45+ minutes of play: 12+ scenes, deep in-episode choices.
- **POV:** Close third on the protagonist. Rare cutaways (once or twice an episode) outside his POV, for dread — never
  revealing her secret.
- **Choices:** Dialogue & tone, moral dilemmas, intimacy beats (small moments with her), strategy (boardroom, deals, leverage).
  Telltale-style cues ("She'll remember that."), no visible numbers during play.
- **Features:** "Previously on" recap built from the player's actual path; character dossiers that update; end card with a
  consequence ledger + one-word relationship temperature per key person; a spoiler-free 3-option pitch menu for the next
  episode (the player picks one).
- **Violence:** writers decide. **Pilot opening:** writers decide. **Title and all names:** writers decide.
- **Hard nos:** No harm to the child (never seriously hurt or endangered on-page). No dream fake-outs.
- **Sexual content limit:** All romantic/sexual characters are adults. Sex is character- and emotion-driven, frank and
  charged in lead-ins and aftermaths, but the act itself happens off the page ("charged then cut"). No graphic explicit
  description. Violence, addiction, grief and other hard issues may be unflinching.
- **Episodic method:** Write one episode at a time. Each new episode builds on the paths the player actually took (read the
  save) rather than pre-building divergent trees across episodes.
