### 2. Places (as described on the page)

- **The Mercy.** Two hundred and twelve thousand acres (Whit, s05). Brand: **R-Bar-M**. Its jet is **Mercy One**.
- **The Chapel at the Mercy.** Cedar and limestone, 120 seats, center aisle, pin lights over the altar, lemon oil and
  cedar smell. Amon and Lorna built it in **2003 on the site of the old stock pens**. The brass plaque by the door reads:
  *THE CHAPEL AT THE MERCY · TO THE GLORY OF GOD AND IN THANKSGIVING FOR THIS LAND · AMON & LORNA RANSOME · MMIII*.
  Mercy Events LLC rents it at **$38,000 a weekend**, linens extra ("A Hill Country Feeling, West Texas Soul"). It
  stands **600 yards west** of the tent. The back door shuts on a pneumatic arm. The **altar step is at the west
  end**, "where the couples stand." There is a west window and a gravel drip bed along the outside of the west wall,
  and a great horned owl in the eaves (s13). `[all]`
- **The House:** the original 1928 limestone ranch house, with a tin roof, a screen door, porch steps and a Scottie-dog
  bootscraper. Its hall smells of saddle soap and Pledge. Paloma knows the creaking boards (the third past the linen
  closet, and the long one at the turn) `[e1_carried_amon]`. The **old room** is Amon's: a narrow iron bed, a saddle on
  a stand, a King James Bible with a rubber band around it, a hat hook, a 1970s Zenith radio, a leather chair, and **a
  baby monitor on the nightstand, the same model as June's** `[e1_carried_amon]` (never explained). The **blue room**
  is at the far east end of the House `[all, s10 and s12b]`. The framed 1986 *Texas Monthly* cover hangs on the hall
  wall (§5).
- **The Italy:** the 1998 stucco "Tuscan" wraparound built around the House. It contains the family wing (triple
  glazing, deep carpet), **June's room**, a gold swan powder room (roped off since four that day because Pete
  Ashworth's nephew was sick in it) `[e1_lied_swan]`, the loggia stairs, and the **twelve-car garage with a Baccarat
  chandelier** Lorna chose in 1998 `[e1_snub_walked]`.
- **The tent:** white sailcloth, the size of a hangar, on the south lawn between the House and the Italy. It has west
  and east flaps, a north service door, a head table (Amon's is the tall chair with arms), Table Two (Lorna), Table
  Nine (the Ashworths), a family table and a board table. The far bar sits under string lights. `[all]`
- **The cutting barn:** north of the tent, floodlit on the party side, with a **$40,000 radiant-heated floor**. Behind
  it, in the dark, are a caliche service road, a pipe fence, black pasture and a clanking windmill. `[all]`
- **The stock tank:** fireworks barge and drone launch. The **water tower** stands to the southeast: white, 140 ft,
  with the R-Bar-M painted on the tank. Beyond the **east fence** runs the county road **FM 2400**. `[all]`
- **Pardee:** two Dollar Generals and a boarded Western Auto `[e1_said_jobs]`. **Ransome Memorial** is the hospital
  `[s08 path]`. The library carries Lorna's name (dossier).
- **San Isidro:** the "downstream village." Paloma says it is **"Four miles downstream. Past the dead pecans."** Whit
  has never driven that road; to him it is a sign he passes. `[all, s09]`

### 3. The company and the money

- **Ransome Energy, Inc.** (NYSE: RNSM). Amon is chairman and CEO, and general partner of **Ransome Family Holdings**,
  which holds the family's **52% of the vote** (dossier). The board has eleven directors. One is a retired ExxonMobil
  executive, two are independents "who go wherever the stock goes," and Pete Ashworth is a member. A UT regent sat at
  Table Six.
- **History on the page:** In **1923 Amon's grandfather** struck oil "a mile from where you're sitting," one month
  after Santa Rita. The family's creed is "Thank God and keep drilling." The company is a hundred and three years old.
  In 1986 the family survived as cattle; in the 2011 drought, as oil. The 1986 cover caption says Amon bought half of
  Midland for pennies the year everyone else went broke. `[all / s06b]`
- **Ransome Horizon** uses the tagline ENERGY FOR THE NEXT TEXAS, written by an Austin agency for $400,000, and its
  drone footage shows a rented pronghorn. **Ransome Midstream** carried the pipe.
- **Project Wellspring** (announced on stage, `[all]`) is a twenty-year, **$3.1 billion** deal. It covers gas-fired
  power from Ransome wells and **nine million gallons a day of groundwater from under the Mercy**, piped to **Halcyon
  Compute's two-gigawatt Lariat Campus**. The PR name is the **Wellspring Community Partnership**. It promises 400
  jobs for Pardee, a new imaging wing at Ransome Memorial, scholarships and a splash pad. Pastor Jimmy Don Quick blessed
  a three-foot length of blue pipe tied with a white satin bow. Greer briefed Whit on the dollar figure in August under
  NDA, but he did not know about the pipe, the pastor or the date. In s06b Greer calls Soren "a man from California
  with a three-billion-dollar check."
- **The Trial** (`[all]`): Amon will not name an heir. "For one year, starting Monday, Hadley and my son will each carry
  the weight of this company… at the end of that year I will name the one who earned it." **Assignments are Monday,
  8 a.m., in Fort Worth. "Wear boots you can walk in."** The winner runs it; the loser works for them "or goes to
  Dallas." Whit's 9:40 acceptance toast never happened.
- **Mercy Ranch Water:** Colt's product. A $9.99 four-pack, "made with San Isidro spring water," with a cartoon
  longhorn in aviators on the can. It is already on sale: it filled the Ball's troughs, and a pallet of 1,200 unopened
  cans was left by the service tent.
- **Ball money:** tables are $25,000, the cause is "rural children's literacy," and the theme is *Boots & Bindings*.
  The centerpieces are hollowed-out classics planted with succulents (Moby-Dick, The Grapes of Wrath, Lonesome Dove).
  The brisket and its pitmaster were flown in from Lockhart, and the pecan pies from Fredericksburg. A Grammy-winning
  Western swing band from Austin played. The drone show cost $180,000 and briefly misspelled **HORZION**. The ice
  sculpture was carved by a man from the Ice Hotel in Sweden.
