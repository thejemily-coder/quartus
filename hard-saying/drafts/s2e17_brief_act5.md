## 5. THE RETURN CONFESSION — THE SPEC (s20–s21; Romance Writer, Opus; the Knowledge Auditor, the Voice Editor and the Standards & Spoiler Guard all sign it; sound Catholic theology throughout)

### 5.0 Rules
1. **POV Jack.** Osei's inner life is never narrated (his hand on his knee, his glasses, his laugh — what Jack sees). Jack's interiority allowed, one sentence of thought then the next thing said.
2. **It is not a confession for most of it** (brief): nothing Jack is distraught about is a sin. **The narration never implies it is.** Ending a relationship honestly is not a sin; loving two people is not a sin; having planned a future is not a sin; the near-kiss that stopped is not a mortal sin (it was temptation, resisted; at most an imprudence — and only if Jack raises it).
