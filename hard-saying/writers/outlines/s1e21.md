## 1. SHAPE

**Title:** **"Guadalupe."** The feast falls on Saturday (Our Lady of Guadalupe, Sat Dec 12, 2009). At the Mañanitas Lena tells Jack the part of the story nobody tells: Juan Diego, late, ashamed, his uncle dying,
**took the long way around the hill so the Lady wouldn't see him** — and she came down the hill anyway and stood in his road: ***¿No estoy yo aquí, que soy tu madre?*** *Am I not here, I who am your mother?* All weekend Jack
takes the other road around everyone who loves him — Theo at dawn, Lena at the party and at her knock and at her text and at the confession line — and all weekend they keep coming down the hill. **Never said in
the narration**; Lena tells the story about Juan Diego and nobody else, and Jack's body answers.

**Dates:** **Friday, December 11 – Sunday, December 13, 2009 (≈10 p.m.).**
- **Fri Dec 11** — last day of classes; St. Damasus (optional). **Tiger Woods announces an indefinite break** (statement on his website — real words only [V time; likely evening ET]); **Notre Dame hires Brian Kelly away from
  undefeated Cincinnati** [V: announced Dec 10–11]; *Invictus* opens; **Hanukkah begins at sundown** (one image at most). Newman Advent party, 7 p.m. Sunset ≈4:48.
- **Sat Dec 12** — **Our Lady of Guadalupe**; Las Mañanitas before dawn; **the Heisman: Mark Ingram, the narrowest margin ever, over Toby Gerhart** [V]; confession 3:30–4:45; Jack's shift 7–1.
- **Sun Dec 13** — **Gaudete Sunday** (Third of Advent; Zeph 3:14–18a; Phil 4:4–7, "Rejoice in the Lord always… the Lord is near"; **Luke 3:10–18, "What then should we do?"** — two cloaks, share one; **rose
  vestments, the rose candle**); **Saints 26, Falcons 23 at Atlanta — 13–0** [V: regulation or OT; the period sheet marks OT UNVERIFIED — the page names only the score and what the Researcher confirms]; Geminids building
  toward Mon's peak [optional, one at most]. Sunrise ≈6:58; first light ≈6:30.
- **Browns 13, Steelers 6 — Thursday, Dec 10** [V; eight sacks of Roethlisberger, recalled] — reaches Jack only in Frank's Saturday voicemail.
**Weather:** Fri clear and cold after Thursday night's spit of rain [V weak]; Sat clear, highs ≈57; Sun dawn cold and still, a small clean swell [V: Central Coast surf Dec 13; water ≈56–57°F]. **Time zones:** Ohio +3; New Orleans +2.

**Logline (spoiler-free):** *Six in the morning and a question about a war, a white elephant with the wrong answers in it, roses before dawn, a new slice at the Anchor — and the coldest water of the year, on purpose.*

**Engine of the episode.** Jack comes home at six in the morning from the bed where he said no, and Theo, awake in the dark, asks why he looks like he's been through a war; Jack can give Theo his own lie back, and
Theo, who has had a question in his calendar since November, moves it instead of asking. On the last day of classes Jack emails Hale the argument of Lena's he'll defend, and lets Skye pick his ten-dollar present for
a party she can't come to — a Magic 8-Ball, because "every answer is a line reading" — and her car dies in a Goleta parking lot, and he arrives at the Newman party an hour late with battery acid on his hands. Everybody
is there. Lena is there with Julian. Laney has done something to the punch. Becca runs the white elephant like a livestock auction; Theo's gift is a can of spray tan and Father Osei gets it; Jack's 8-Ball tells Joey
Abruzzo he will be a priest, and then Lena steals it, and Jack opens a can of New Orleans coffee with *—L.* on the tag. Lena wins an argument about "Joy to the World" with one fact; Gus asks if anybody plays, and
Jack, who has heard Lena play around a dead F at one in the morning, keeps her secret; Julian plays instead, beautifully, and kisses her cheek under contraband mistletoe, and Osei confiscates it. At a quarter to
eleven Lena offers him a ride and he walks the other way. At Sol's falling-down house on Del Playa, Skye asks, plainly, whether it's still *right now*, and tells him to say no again, slower — and he does, and she
keeps to the letter of it and to nothing else, and it is the hardest hour of his life so far. At five in the morning she drives him "for the light" to the Mañanitas and goes south; inside, under the roses, Lena
says *Theo said you were at the library*, and then tells him about a man who took the other road. That afternoon he kneels behind a linen screen and tells a priest about a door he left open, and Osei tells him what
a no that's only for tonight costs: it has to be said again tomorrow. That night Skye sits at his counter, Gloria rates her boysenberry and keeps a stranger's phone out of her face, and Lena texts *Pie?* while Skye
watches him read it. At one in the morning Skye offers him the sleeping way — and whichever road he takes, they meet at Ellery at first light on Gaudete Sunday, and she tries to teach him to surf in December, and
he is terrible, and it's the happiest he has been in a week. Then the Saints are thirteen and oh — at Sully's with Lena, or in a text while he watches Skye eat pancakes without checking the angle once — and at
the 9 p.m. Mass the rose candle is lit, and on the steps Lena shakes the 8-Ball she stole, reads it, laughs, and shows him: **Better not tell you now.** **The episode is about being there in body — and about who keeps
coming down the hill.**

**Register.** Warmth first: Theo in the dark; a Rite Aid at sundown; a push-start; the best ensemble room since Halloween (Becca's auction chant, Theo's spray tan, Tyler grieving Brian Kelly, Mrs. Fitch's descant on

with *—L.*; "Ride with us?"; *Theo said you were at the library*; the other road; "Can you?"; *Pie?*; "I don't count"; the bell rope going to Dale; *Better not tell you now.* Heat once, hard (s07a), carrying character;
