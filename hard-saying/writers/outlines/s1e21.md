## 1. SHAPE

**Title:** **"Guadalupe."** The feast falls on Saturday (Our Lady of Guadalupe, Sat Dec 12, 2009). At the Mañanitas Lena tells Jack the part of the story nobody tells: Juan Diego, late, ashamed, his uncle dying,
**took the long way around the hill so the Lady wouldn't see him** — and she came down the hill anyway and stood in his road: ***¿No estoy yo aquí, que soy tu madre?*** *Am I not here, I who am your mother?* All weekend Jack
takes the other road around everyone who loves him — Theo at dawn, Lena at the party and at her knock and at her text, the confession line, a priest with a wrench — and all weekend they keep coming down the hill. **Never said in
the narration**; Lena tells the story about Juan Diego and nobody else, and Jack's body answers.

**Dates:** **Friday, December 11 – Sunday, December 13, 2009 (≈10 p.m.).**
- **Fri Dec 11** — last day of classes; St. Damasus (optional). **Tiger Woods announces an indefinite break** on his website (real words only; time [UNVERIFIED — "Friday afternoon"]); **Notre Dame introduces Brian Kelly** (from undefeated
  Cincinnati) [V]; *Invictus* opens; **Hanukkah begins at sundown** (one image at most). Newman Advent party, 7 p.m. Sunset ≈4:48. Morning wet pavement; **no rain during the party** (period §10).

  cold, windy-bright.

**Logline (spoiler-free):** *Six in the morning and a question about a war, a white elephant with the wrong answers in it, roses before dawn, a new slice at the Anchor — and the coldest water of the year, on purpose.*

**Engine of the episode.** Jack comes home at six in the morning from the bed where he said no, and Theo, awake in the dark, asks why he looks like he's been through a war; Jack can give Theo his own lie back, and Theo, who has
had a question in his calendar since November, moves it instead of asking. On the last day of classes Jack emails Hale the argument of Lena's he'll defend, hears his father tell a voicemail that the Browns beat Pittsburgh, and
answers Lena's Thursday-night text or doesn't; then Skye spends the afternoon casting his ten-dollar present for a party she can't come to — a Magic 8-Ball, because "every answer is a line reading" — and her car dies in a parking
lot, and he arrives at the Newman party an hour late with battery acid on his hands. Everybody is there. Lena is there with Julian. Laney has done something to the punch. Becca runs the white elephant like a cattle auction;
Theo's spray tan goes to Father Osei; Jack's 8-Ball tells Joey Abruzzo he will be a priest, and then Lena steals it, and Jack opens a can of New Orleans coffee with *—L.* on the tag. Lena wins an argument about "Joy to the
World" with one fact; Gus asks if anybody plays, and Jack, who has heard Lena play around a dead F at one in the morning, keeps her secret; Julian plays instead, beautifully, and kisses her cheek under contraband mistletoe, and
Osei confiscates it. At the sink Osei washes cups and Jack dries, and the priest tells him what a guest who leaves before the singing is finished is, in Kumasi, and where his door is. At a quarter to eleven Lena offers him a
ride and he walks the other way. At Sol's falling-down house Skye asks, plainly, whether it's still *right now*, and tells him to say no again, slower — and he does, and she keeps to the letter of it and to nothing else, and it is
the hardest hour of his life so far, and then the fuse goes. At five in the morning she drives him "for the light" to the Mañanitas and goes south; inside, under the roses, Lena says *Theo said you were at the library*, and then
tells him about a man who took the other road. That afternoon, in the confession line beside her, his pocket buzzes — *for AFTER* — and he either kneels behind a linen screen and tells a priest about a door he left open, or gets
out of line and sits an hour on a wall until the priest comes out with a wrench. That night Skye sits at his counter in his shirt, Gloria rates her boysenberry and keeps a stranger's phone out of her face, and Lena texts *Pie?*
while Skye watches him read it. At one in the morning Skye says she's going to ask every day, and offers him the sleeping way — and whichever road he takes, they meet at Ellery at first light on Gaudete Sunday, and she tries to
teach him to surf in December, and he is terrible, and it's the happiest he has been in a week. The Saints go thirteen and oh. Sol films Theo's shoes and asks what's in the black bag. At the 9 p.m. Mass the rose candle is lit,
and on the steps Lena shakes the 8-Ball she stole, reads it, laughs, and shows him: **Better not tell you now.** **The episode is about being there in body — and about who keeps coming down the hill.**
