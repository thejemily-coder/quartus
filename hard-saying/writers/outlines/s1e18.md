## 1. SHAPE

**Title:** **"Visiting Hours."** St. Elizabeth's (two to eight; a discharge on a holiday morning), Trumbull Correctional (a special visit, Friday morning), and a house on
Glenwood Avenue that takes in a visitor for four days and puts him in the empty chair and the empty bed. Gina's old taunt — "the class expert on visiting the imprisoned" —
answered by the family itself. Never said in the narration except as a sign on a wall. Danny's line to Theo ("Somebody oughta come see *you*") is the title's knife.

**Dates:** **Wednesday, November 25 (evening) – Sunday, November 29, 2009.** Wed: PIT ≈7:20 p.m. ET (forty minutes late out of Denver — E17 s24); Glenwood ≈8:50. Thu:
Thanksgiving; Packers 34, Lions 12 (12:30 ET on the front-room TV); Cowboys 24, Raiders 7 (4:15); Broncos 26, Giants 6 (night; the garage radio) [V]; *Sweetwater* Thursdays at
nine — **a rerun** (the room's invention; never assert a real network's schedule). Fri: **Tiger Woods' crash** (~2:25 a.m., Windermere; the morning news — crash and hydrant
only); the **Miraculous Medal feast** (Lena's text). Sat: the Advent vigil. **Sun: First Sunday of Advent, Year C** — Jer 33:14–16; 1 Thess 3:12–4:2; **Luke 21:25–28, 34–36**
("Be vigilant at all times and pray…"; "stand erect and raise your heads"); the wreath, the first violet candle, no Gloria, "And also with you"; **Bengals 16, Browns 7** (1–10)
[V]. Return flight Sun evening, PIT → (one change) → SBA (Researcher). **Do not use:** Obama's West Point speech (Tue Dec 1), Saints–Patriots (Mon Nov 30), Tiger's apology or
infidelity (Dec 2 on), *Jersey Shore* (Dec 3), the bowl pairings (Dec 6).
**Weather:** gray, damp, 30s–40s; **no snow** (the sheet supports none — *is it snowing* gets *its not snowing* / *describe the not snowing*). Sunset ≈5:00 p.m.; a fat half-moon
sets ≈11 p.m.–midnight. **Time zones:** Ohio Eastern; Skye Pacific (−3); Lena Central (−1); Becca Pacific (−3).

**Logline (spoiler-free):** *Thanksgiving on Glenwood Avenue: a grandmother with one good hand, a guest asked to say grace, two hours without a phone in a room with
vending machines — and a phone that won't stop buzzing at the table.*

**Engine of the episode.** For four days Theo Vance — who has never been anybody's guest without a reservation — is taken into a family with a hole in the middle of it, and the
family, without a word of discussion, fits him into the hole: Danny's bed, Danny's chair, the grace, a big brother for Gina, a pair of hands for a grandmother with a broken wrist,
a man in the garage for Frank. Then on Friday Jack takes him to meet the man whose place he's been keeping warm — and Danny, in an hour, sees the thing the Costigans were too
kind to say: that the rich kid pays for everything so nobody notices nobody came. Meanwhile, in Jack's pocket, a girl in Los Angeles who is on television Thursdays at nine texts
him through every meal, and Jack — with exactly one co-conspirator, his fifteen-year-old sister — keeps her in his own house, from everyone, including the roommate who (🔒) knows
her best. **The episode is about what a family holds without saying.** Nonna says one sentence at a hospital chair and asks for her slippers. Danny lies about a swan. Frank sits
in the truck. Theo prays for a table that isn't his, and puts supermarket flowers at the feet of a plaster Madonna before dawn. And Lena calls from a loud house in New Orleans,
and it's warm, and it's short, and he hangs up wanting more of it than either of them has a right to.

**Register.** Warmth first (the curb; Maria's kitchen at nine at night; Gina in the basement, sworn and vibrating; Nonna conscripting Theo; the table) — comedy all through
(Gina's translations, Theo's wrong Italian, the peach pie, the RAZR buzzing in Maria's apron through dessert, T9 against a girl who types like a hummingbird). The knife comes in
small cuts: Nonna's sentence (Thursday morning), the grace and "Da stasera, io" (Thursday afternoon), Danny's line (Friday), the silent truck and the question ("Does he ever say
he's sorry?" — "No."). Thursday night is tender and a little guilty (Lena's voice; the buzz against his ear). Saturday night is heat, in words, in a cold basement. Sunday is
Advent: be vigilant; a wreath; Gina offering a hand across an aisle; a man in a garage telling a rich boy to come back; a twenty pressed into a palm.

**Cadence check.** **Two-hander with Lena: none** — the brief's E18 note (in touch regularly, not as deeply as Christmas) governs; the call is a warm ≈1,300-word phone scene,
the Broussards loud, Lena alone for three minutes, then gone. The every-other cadence resumes at **E20** (see §12 — the E19–E22 design round puts Julian's song in E20). **Ensemble
setpiece:** the Glenwood Thanksgiving (family, not campus; s05–s06). **Duel:** none. Campus ensemble by phone and text only (Laney via Theo; Kevin, Margot one text each —
optional; Becca one text).

**Length:** **≈21,000 words per path (ceiling ~22,000; Skye exception).** **23 choices per path**; 10–11 keys; 6 cues; 5 needle-drops; **2 genuine forks** (`e18_night`
Thursday; `e18_sat` Saturday) — both converge.
