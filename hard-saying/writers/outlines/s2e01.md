## 1. SHAPE

**Title:** **"Midnight."** Midnight Mass at St. Brigid's; midnight in New Orleans an hour after his; midnight Pacific at three in the morning in Ohio; the shortest day of the year on Monday and the longest night on the
calendar after it. Every important call in the week comes at an hour nobody keeps. Never said as a theme.

**Dates:** **Saturday, December 19 – Friday, December 25, 2009.** Cold open ≈4:55 p.m. EST Saturday (PIT); the last scene 11:58 p.m. EST Friday (Glenwood Avenue).
- **Sat Dec 19** — *O Radix*; Benedict's decrees (JPII, Pius XII Venerable) [V]; the Mid-Atlantic blizzard (PIT: snow; delays plausible) [V]; **Saints–Cowboys, Superdome, Saturday night (NFL Network)**: Dallas led 24–3 entering the
  fourth; the Saints scored twice; Nick Folk's late 24-yarder hit the upright; the last drive ended on a sack-fumble; **Dallas 24, New Orleans 17** [V — period sheet E22 §1.3; kickoff ≈7:20 p.m. CST UNVERIFIED]. Tess's daughter born at
  Touro (bible canon), named **Lucille** (Lena godmother).
- **Sun Dec 20** — Fourth Sunday of Advent: Micah 5:1–4a; Heb 10:5–10; **Luke 1:39–45, the Visitation** ("the infant leaped in her womb for joy"); *O Clavis*; **Browns 41, Chiefs 34** at Kansas City — Harrison 286 rushing yards, Cribbs two
  kickoff-return TDs [V]; Steelers 37, Packers 36 (the last play) [V]; Brittany Murphy dies [V — background only].
- **Mon Dec 21** — the solstice [V time]; *O Oriens*; Margot turns 19; Hale's marks post ("Marks post Monday" — E22).
- **Tue Dec 22** — *O Rex*; **Skye's party in the hills** ("everybody I went to middle school with" — E22 s07a 🔒) — Theo is there.
- **Wed Dec 23** — *O Emmanuel*; Tariq comes home (fourteen stairs — E22 s09); Lena's package arrives.
- **Thu Dec 24** — **Senate passes health care 60–39 ≈7:05 a.m.** [V]; Christmas Eve confessions at St. Brigid's (fictional schedule, 10–11:30); **St. Peter's Midnight Mass begins 10 p.m. Rome = 4 p.m. EST; the woman in red vaults the
  barrier; Benedict down and up; Cardinal Etchegaray injured** [V]; La Vigilia (the Seven Fishes); **the bonfires on the levee in St. James Parish lit ≈7 p.m. CST** [V custom]; Midnight Mass at St. Brigid's (carols 11:30).
- **Fri Dec 25** — Christmas; Isaiah 9 / Luke 2 at midnight; **Northwest 253 (Amsterdam–Detroit) — the attempted bombing; landed ≈11:53 a.m. EST; on the news by afternoon** [V]; Chargers at Titans on TV [V — background]; Theo's
  flight to LAX that evening (unnamed carrier).
**Time zones:** Youngstown = Eastern; New Orleans −1; LA −3.

**Logline (spoiler-free):** *Christmas week on Glenwood Avenue: a game on two televisions, a guest who isn’t company anymore, a page somebody finds — and a phone that won’t stay in the basket.*

**Engine of the episode.** Jack comes down out of the snow at Pittsburgh with Lena's night still on him and a picture in his pocket, and his father has been parked since half past two with a thermos and the Browns hat. Saturday night the
Costigans' cable doesn't get the game, so Frank drives him to a bar on Market Street and orders a ginger ale, and Lena calls from a maternity waiting room at Touro where her whole family is yelling at a television bolted to the wall; her
sister has the baby in the third quarter, while they're still undefeated, and names her for Grandmère; then the Saints lose, and Lena laughs and cries in the same breath two thousand miles away, and the 8-Ball says *Very doubtful*. Sunday
morning the first innocent picture ever to land on his phone is a newborn, and he has to open it at the kitchen table with his mother at his shoulder and five other pictures underneath it. His father's Browns run for two hundred and
eighty-six yards and his father laughs in the garage. Monday his mother reads the phone bill line by line — four picture messages, a dollar — and Theo calls from LA to ask if he can come for Christmas Eve, and Frank shouts from the garage
that he doesn't have to call first. Tuesday Gina interrogates him through the Southern Park Mall, and he mails Lena something, or doesn't, and mails a money order to a woman in Struthers, eleven minutes away. Tuesday night, in the hills
above Los Angeles, Theo walks out onto a terrace and finds Skye Callender; at twenty to three Wednesday morning Skye calls Jack in his father's cold garage and does Theo's voice, perfectly, and tells him she's known his roommate since
they were eleven, and that Ted knows, and that Ted was *careful*, and Ted is only careful when he's protecting somebody; she makes him stand barefoot in the snow by the bathtub Madonna and do the weather. Christmas Eve at seven in the
morning the Senate passes health care on the truck radio and his father says *Merry Christmas*; Theo comes off the red-eye and Frank hands him the parka without a word, and Theo knows Jack knows, or doesn't, depending on what Jack
says about the hills. At four the Pope goes down on the kitchen television and gets up again, and Lena texts inside a minute — *Is he alright? Are you?* — and Skye texts an hour later to ask if he was Jack's favorite. At La Vigilia
Nonna sets a place for Danny and nobody moves it, Frank tells Rosie to say grace because Theo isn't company, and the phones go in the bread basket, and the basket buzzes, and Theo gets there before Rosie and hands it down the table
face-down: *Yours.* At eight Lena holds her phone up to a bonfire on the levee so he can hear it burn, and he stands in the snow by the Madonna and listens. At midnight Carly Benedetto turns around two pews up to give him the peace.
At three Skye wants the snow globe described, and Theo, in Danny's bed, says *Merry Christmas, Costigan* into the dark, and Jack decides whether to say *She told me.* Christmas morning Gina's present for him is the second season of
*Sweetwater*, FOR RESEARCH, opened in front of everybody, and Nonna points at the girl in the blazer on the cover and says *L'attrice!* — and by eight the whole family is around the Dell at the kitchen table, all the way out, and all of
them asking about Lena. Danny calls from Trumbull and laughs until the system cuts him off in the middle of a sentence about the veil girl. Theo flies home into a country that's just learned about a man on a plane. And on Christmas night,
in the garage, Lena lists every dish at Réveillon, and the chair they set for her grandmother, and then says, for the first time, *My mama worked at Memorial* — and nothing more — and Jack has to decide what to do with a grief he can't
argue. At midnight Nonna, awake in her chair, looks at the phone in his hand and asks him a question in Italian. **The episode is about being known — by a house, a priest, a roommate, a family, a television — and the one person who
knows him best being the one person who knows nothing.**

**Register.** Warmth first and almost all the way through: snow, fish, the Browns' best day in years, a newborn named for the dead, Theo in the parka eating an eel on a bet, Rosie's grace, Gina's DVD, Frank's ginger ale, Nonna's
Calabrese, the levee on fire, Midnight Mass. The knife in small cuts: *four picture messages*; *Ted was careful*; the basket; *Is he alright? Are you?* next to *was he your favorite*; Danny's empty place; Carly's hand; *Is she Catholic,
at least?*; the cut-off line; *My mama worked at Memorial.*; Nonna's question. **Bittersweet comedy**, the brief's "proxy war, darkly funny while sad," at its gentlest before January.

**Cadence check.** Family set piece (the episode is one): La Vigilia (s12) and Christmas morning (s16–s16a). **The Jack/Lena long scene: s12a + s18** (two phone scenes, ≈3,000 words — the two-hander rule kept by phone; S2E03 carries
