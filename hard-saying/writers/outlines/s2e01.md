and has been told to ask one question. Saturday night the Costigans' cable doesn't carry the game, so Frank drives him to a bar on Market Street and orders a ginger ale, and Lena — who made him promise to watch it on the phone from
the garage — calls from Touro's maternity waiting room with her whole family yelling at a set bolted to the wall; her sister has the baby in the third quarter, while they're still undefeated, and names her for both grandmothers; then
the Saints lose, and Lena laughs and cries in one breath, and the 8-Ball says *Very doubtful*. At twenty to one the first innocent picture ever to reach his phone is a newborn, and his mother wants to see it at the kitchen table with
five others underneath it. Sunday after the Visitation he drives to Trumbull with his mother, and while she takes a very long time at the vending machines he tells his brother the truth — that he's in love with the girl who asked
Danny's name, that she's with a good man who wrote her a song, that he disappeared for a month into a girl from California who makes him happy, that both of those are true — and Danny, who doesn't wish, tells him he isn't going to say
which one, and then says the sentence Jack drives home with. The Browns run for two hundred and eighty-six yards and his father laughs in the garage. Monday Theo calls from LA and Jack answers on the first ring, and Theo says *You
answered*, and asks for Christmas Eve, and Frank shouts through the garage door that he doesn't have to call first. Tuesday Gina, who has kept a secret twenty-seven days, interrogates him through the Southern Park Mall, and he mails
Lena something, or doesn't, and mails a money order to a woman in Struthers, eleven minutes away. Tuesday night in the hills above Los Angeles Theo walks onto a terrace and finds Skye Callender, and at twenty to three Wednesday
morning, in the basement next to the baccalà, Skye does his roommate's voice so well Jack knows it before she says the name — she's known Ted since they were eleven, Ted knows, and Ted was *careful*, and Ted is only careful when he's
protecting somebody — and makes him go up the stairs and out the side door barefoot into the old snow to do the weather. Christmas Eve the Senate passes health care on the truck radio and his father says *Merry Christmas*; Theo comes
off the red-eye and Frank hands him the parka without a word; at four the Pope goes down on the kitchen television and gets up, and Lena texts inside a minute — *Is he alright? Are you?* At La Vigilia Nonna sets a place for Danny and
nobody moves it, the phones go in the bread basket, the basket buzzes, and Theo gets there before Rosie and hands it down face-down: *Yours.* At eight Jack takes the phone to the garage, as he said he would, and Lena holds hers up to a
bonfire on the levee so he can hear it burn. At midnight a girl he went to school with turns around two pews up to give him the peace. At three Skye wants the snow globe described, and Theo in Danny's bed says *Merry Christmas,
Costigan* into the dark. Christmas morning it's raining, and Gina's present is the second season of *Sweetwater*, FOR RESEARCH, opened in front of everybody, and Nonna points at the girl in the blazer and says *L'attrice!* — and by
eight the whole family is around the Dell at the kitchen table, all the way out, and all of them asking about Lena; and Skye calls him on the porch glider and is delighted beyond words that his family knows her as the nice one. Danny
