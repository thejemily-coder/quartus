# COLD OPEN

### s01 · Cold open · "The Pacific"
- **slug:** SATURDAY · 6:52 AM · CALIFORNIA STATE ROUTE 1, NORTH OF MARISOL
- **music:** Bruce Springsteen — "Youngstown"
- **beats:**
  1. Open in the cab of a forest-green 1999 Chevy Silverado, 2,400 miles from home, rust blooming over the rear wheel
     wells, 212,000 miles, the defroster that only works on high. Frank Costigan (51; UAW Local 1112 windbreaker; Folgers in
     a Thermos) has driven through the night from Bakersfield. Springsteen on the CD player — the song about their town;
     Frank sings the steel-mill line under his breath, badly, and then, embarrassed, punches over to AM for the coverage
     from Boston: they're burying Ted Kennedy this morning.
  2. Frank's two-sentence eulogy: *"He was our guy. And he was wrong about the babies."* That's the Costigan politics in a
     nutshell; Jack (the narration) loves him for it and for being unable to say more.
  3. The marine layer. Then the road bends and the fog tears open and there it is: the Pacific. Frank pulls into a gravel
     turnout without signaling. He has never seen it. He gets out and stands at the guardrail in his windbreaker with his
     hands in his pockets, fifty-one years old, and doesn't say anything for a while. Jack watches his father look at an
     ocean.
  4. Frank hands Jack an envelope: $340 in twenties. "Two hundred's from your nonna. Hundred's from your mother. Forty's
     from me — don't tell your mother there's a forty." Jack knows (narration, plainly) that the SUB pay runs out in
     October. He knows there's a pillow and a blanket behind the truck's bench seat that weren't there in Youngstown.
  5. Frank: "Your brother wanted me to tell you something." Beat. "He said, and I'm quoting, 'Tell the Professor don't be a
     pussy.'" Frank laughs; it catches; he coughs it into his fist. *(Elision rule: the narration does not explain where
     Danny is. Jack touches his St. Michael medal. "Danny was my sponsor" is NOT said here.)*
- **choices:**
  - **[Sincerity]** "Thanks for driving me, Dad. All of it." → set `trait.sincerity +1`, `soul.honesty +1`,
    `e1_dad_thanked: true`. **key:** "You thanked your father at the edge of the Pacific." Response: Frank, gruff: "It's
    a truck. It drives." Then, not looking at him: "Your grandfather would've liked this. He'd have hated it, but he'd
    have liked it."
  - **[Wit]** "It's just Lake Erie with a tan." → `trait.wit +1`, `e1_dad_joked: true`. Response: Frank barks a laugh.
    "Don't let your mother hear you. She thinks Erie's a resort." The joke lets them both off the hook, which is what jokes
    are for in the Costigan family.
  - **[Candor]** "Take the money back. You need it more than I need California." → `trait.candor +1`, `soul.honesty +1`,
    `e1_dad_offered_home: true`. Response: Frank's face closes like a garage door. *"Don't you ever say that. Not after
    what it — "* He stops. "You're here. That's what it was for. Don't waste it." (Seed: "what it cost." 🔒 Danny.)
- **next:** s02 (all choices goto s02)
- **journal:** person `frank` — "Dad" — "Frank Costigan. Twenty-six years on the line at Lordstown, laid off since June.
  Drove me 2,400 miles because two plane tickets cost more than the truck is worth. Saw the Pacific for the first time
  today. Didn't say anything. That's how you know."
- **Title card** follows s01 (engine handles).
