## 1. SHAPE

**Title:** **"Sometime."** In a drugstore aisle on St. Nicholas Eve Lena asked "Where do you go?" and Jack said *I'll tell you sometime. Not yet.* — and she said okay, and never asked again. The finale is the night he nearly
tells her, and the night she nearly tells him something else, and the morning they part on a promise neither of them makes out loud — while in a car on a bluff he has said yes to January. *Sometime* is the season's hope and its
sting in one word: not now. **Never said in the narration as a theme**; spoken once, by Jack, on the porch (s14).
