## 1. SHAPE

**Title:** **"Sometime."** (Retitled from the misdated provisional "Gaudete" — Gaudete Sunday is Dec 13, inside E21.) In a drugstore aisle on St. Nicholas Eve Lena asked "Where do you go?" and Jack said *I'll tell you
sometime. Not yet.* — and she said okay, and never asked again. The finale is the night he nearly tells her, and the night she nearly tells him something else, and the morning they part on a promise neither of them
makes out loud. *Sometime* is the season's hope and its sting in one word: not now. **Never said in the narration as a theme**; the word is spoken once, by Jack, on the porch (s14), and the end card says the 8-Ball's
version of it.

**Dates:** **Monday, December 14 – Saturday, December 19, 2009** (the cold open is 12:58 a.m. Monday; the last scene is ≈11:05 a.m. MST Saturday, Denver).
- **Mon Dec 14** — finals begin; **the Geminids peak overnight Sun–Mon** (near-new-moon dark sky; best shower of the year [V calc]).
- **Tue Dec 15** — the D.C. Council votes 11–2 for same-sex marriage; the Archdiocese warns about Catholic Charities' city contracts [V] (one line at most, Thursday — §3 s09, optional).
- **Wed Dec 16** — **new moon**; **Hale's oral, 2 p.m., Hollis 314**; confession 8 p.m. at St. Brendan's (continuity §1.7).
- **Thu Dec 17** — the **O Antiphons** begin at Vespers (*O Sapientia*); Copenhagen's heads of state arrive (background only).
- **Fri Dec 18** — the last finals; **the Ferrer carillon plays "Hotel California" at noon** (tradition — bible §25.1); ***Avatar* opens**; **GM announces it will wind down Saab** [V]; Copenhagen ends; *O Adonai*.
  Sunset ≈4:50; residence halls close Sat noon [V master: "Dec 19–20"].
- **Sat Dec 19** — *O Radix Jesse*; the shuttle 4:45 a.m.; SBA ≈5:40; sunrise ≈7:03 [V]; Denver ≈11:00 MST. (Off-page and after the episode: **Saints–Cowboys at the Superdome, Saturday night — the first loss**; Benedict XVI
  declares John Paul II "Venerable" [V — optional, one image on a Denver concourse TV, cut-first]; the East Coast blizzard [V — reaches the Valley? Researcher].)
**Time zones:** Ohio +3; New Orleans +2; Denver +1.

**Logline (spoiler-free):** *Finals week: twenty minutes inside somebody else's argument, one in the morning in a car with no future — and the last night, every place it happened, until the shuttle comes.*

**Engine of the episode.** At one in the morning on the night of the Geminids, Jack lies on the cold lawn under Delgado with Skye in his ear from a balcony in Studio City, where there are no stars, describing the meteors
to her one at a time — and four stories above him on the roof are all his friends, and Lena, who texted him to come up, and whose call beeps in while he's on the other line. He lets it go, or he lifts a hand to her
from the lawn with the phone still at his ear. On Tuesday night, in the Saab at the Marine Lab turnout, Skye plays Professor Hale from his description well enough to frighten him, and drills him on the argument he has to
defend — and tells him he isn't defending an argument, he's defending a person; and asks, because it's Tuesday; and at the corner his phone comes back on to Lena asking whether he wants to practice — *I'll be Hale.*
On Wednesday at two, in Hale's office with a deaf greyhound on the rug, Lena defends Jack's best sentence for twenty minutes — *a man knows his own heart by what he says yes to* — and wins it, and turns it, once, very
quietly, on him; then Jack defends hers against Hale, and Hale, who said he would know, knows. At eight he kneels in the box or walks past the light. At one in the morning, in a car GM will kill in a day and a half,
Skye doesn't ask; she tells him what they're going to be in January, in her voice, and he says yes in one of four ways, and she admits she's been counting. Julian kisses Lena goodbye at his car while Jack watches through
the Fishbowl glass and asks him to make sure she eats. Margot turns nineteen two days early under protest. Laney reads everybody's departure times out loud until the only names left are Jack's and Lena's, and Kevin says
leave it alone. On Friday the tower plays "Hotel California" off-key; Theo tears up the tab and leaves his cashmere on Jack's bed "for Ohio"; Margot lights a candle "for research"; Rafi won't take his bike back; GM
pulls the plug on Saab and Skye texts that he's on the hook. By dusk the building is empty, and Lena knocks on 412 expecting nobody — and he's there. She plays the basement piano for him properly, because he never
asked. They walk: the lounge, St. Brendan's — where Father Osei blesses them both for the road and Lena tells him the secret in the O Antiphons — the 11 bus, a dark Bastille, the Anchor and an argument about whether God
cares who wins, the beach back west, the porch where they met, where Jack nearly tells her where he goes and can't. At the Point, for the first time all semester, she walks past the last house, and shows him a photograph a
dead girl's mother sent her, and says she was asleep that night, and touches her medal — and Jack has held that jacket. They sit on the Lagoon bench until the cold gets into them, and go up on the roof three nights
before the longest night, and he nearly says it, and she stops him, and asks the 8-Ball something she won't tell him: *Ask again later.* Priya takes the roll at 4:40 — Costigan, present. In the dark of the Santa Barbara
apron Lena turns at the foot of the airstairs and says two words through the glass he can't read. Her plane goes; his goes. In Denver his phone comes on and won't stop: his sister, Lena — *fourteen and oh, it's ordained* —
and, last, Skye. **The episode is about saying it — and every way two people find not to.**

**Register.** Warmth first, and the warmest night of the season at its center: Skye in his ear naming helicopters as stars; Skye's Hale; Lena's twenty minutes; Julian's decency; Laney's cake and Margot's speech;
the tower; Theo's tab; the basement piano; Osei on the steps; Gloria's pecan; a theology of field goals; the porch, laughing; the roof; Priya's roll; the van radio; the tin and the holy card. The knife in small cuts: the call
waiting; *I'll be Hale*; "where he says yes at two in the morning"; "Thursday. Not asking."; a kiss at a car; "And Priya."; "Julian's coming for New Year's"; "You asked me where I go —"; "I was asleep"; the jacket; *Ask again
later*; two words through glass; the phone that won't keep still. **Bittersweet, not tragic** — the hope is real and the player should feel it.

**Cadence check.** **The extended Jack/Lena two-hander: s11–s17 + s19** (the walk — the finale's heart; ≈9,300 words; brief: an extended two-hander roughly every other episode — E19 → E22 by design). Ensemble set piece: **Margot's
