**Logline (spoiler-free):** *A bill comes due, home calls at one in the morning, somebody stays to the end of a song, and a week is up.*

week she is being asked, gently, by a better-mannered man than Jack has ever been. Every scene between them is warm and honest. The player

the courtyard wall) plus Sully's and the 9 p.m. Mass — the brief's "never cut Lena back too much" holds. Setpiece: none (Bastille is a mid-size
room). Duel: **one, unofficial** (the official record stays where E14 left it on every path — Margot keeps this one on her whiteboard instead).
