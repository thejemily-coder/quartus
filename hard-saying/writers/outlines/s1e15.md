## 1. SHAPE

**Title:** "Black Monday." Three Mondays under one word: Nonno's Black Monday (Sheet & Tube, Sept 19, 1977 — on the page since E13); **the Monday
prayers at the Nikolaikirche in Leipzig** (the duel); and **the Monday night Frank Costigan doesn't come home.** Never explained on the page.

**Dates:** Monday, November 9 – Sunday, November 15, 2009. Berlin Wall 20th (Mon; the giant dominoes toppled at the Brandenburg Gate that evening
Berlin time); Steelers 28, Broncos 10 (MNF, Mon); Obama at the Fort Hood memorial (Tue, 10:55 a.m. PST); Veterans Day / St. Martin of Tours (Wed);
**Yusuf's 19th birthday (Thu Nov 12 — bible §10.11)**; Café Bastille open mic (Thu); Friday the 13th (sunset ≈4:47 p.m.); **Ohio State 27, Iowa
24 (OT), Sat Nov 14, Columbus** (kickoff UNVERIFIED — see s19); **Saints 28, Rams 23 at St. Louis, Sun Nov 15, 10:00 a.m. PST → 9–0** (Roby's 97-yd
kickoff return to open the second half; Meachem's 27-yd TD early in the fourth; Brees 2 INTs); **33rd Sunday in Ordinary Time, Year B** (Dan
12:1–3; Heb 10:11–14, 18; **Mark 13:24–32**). New moon Mon Nov 16: **no moon Sunday night**, stars, Jupiter bright in the south. Dry, clear, mild
all week (climatology; no weather beats).

**Logline (spoiler-free):** *A bill comes due, home calls at one in the morning, somebody stays to the end of a song, and a week is up.*

**Engine of the episode.** E14 was Jack being there for her. E15 is Lena being there for him — his father, his sister, his money — in the same
week she is being asked, gently, by a better-mannered man than Jack has ever been. Every scene between them is warm and honest. At noon Friday,
under the eucalyptus, she mentions it on the way to something else. Jack smiles and says the right thing, and then spends a weekend being
excellent company. The people who love him and aren't in love with him can tell. The one who is in love with him can't.

**Register.** Comedy front-loaded (the Wall duel, Theo's Hasselhoff, Laney's open-mic scorecards, Mrs. Lindell's jar FOR THE CATHOLIC ROOMMATE),
dread in the middle (one in the morning on the 4th-floor landing; Gina's text; the Folgers can), a sweet Thursday that turns on one line ("Can I walk
you back?"), a Friday noon that turns on one aside, and a weekend that is very funny, which is the worst part.

**Cadence check.** Two-hander: **none owed** (E14 had it; E16 is due). Lena still carries five real scenes (the duel; the bluff bench; Bastille; the
Grove; the courtyard wall) plus Sully's and the 9 p.m. Mass — "never cut Lena back too much" holds. Setpiece: none. Duel: **one, unofficial** (`wall`;
Margot's whiteboard; **the record card is not touched**).

**Length:** **≈17,000 words per path (hard ceiling 18,000; written ≈23,500 all branches).** Not a Skye episode — the 22k exception does not apply.
**20 choices per path** (21 on the duel-apology path); 7–12 keys; 6 cue placements (1–6 per path); 5 needle-drops.
