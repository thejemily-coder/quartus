## 1. SHAPE

**Title:** **"A Good Man"** (moved here with the Skye meet; O'Connor's title, cut in half). Becca was one and it wasn't the thing; Julian is one and it may not be
enough; Margot says so; the Misfit says there's no real pleasure in life; the grandmother, at the end, calls a murderer her child; and Jack — the brief's good man who

**Dates:** **Thursday, November 19 – Saturday, November 21, 2009** (tag ≈5:45 p.m. Sat). Karzai sworn in; the UC Regents' fee vote at UCLA (Thu — TV, not Marisol);
***New Moon* midnight shows Thu → Fri** (record $26.3M midnight [V]); the Wheeler Hall occupation at Berkeley (Fri — TV); **the Manhattan Declaration** (Fri; online

straight (2004–09); Senate cloture 60–39 (Sat night). Saints idle (9–0). Moon: a thin crescent sets by ~8:30 p.m. Thu and Fri — **no moon after midnight**; the Leonids
are done. Sunrise Sat ≈6:33; first gray ≈5:45. Dry, clear, mild; cool nights (climatology only — **no rain beat**).

**Logline (spoiler-free):** *Two bad days, a piano nobody admits to playing, a town full of vampires — and a party in the hills where somebody has Flannery O'Connor
exactly backwards.*

**Engine of the episode.** E15 was a decent man doing the hard thing. E16 is what it costs him, which nobody is allowed to see: he tells everyone it's Becca, and
the two people who love him most and aren't in love with him know better in one look. Thursday night Lena is warmer than she has ever been — a basement, a book, a
piece of music with a hole in it — and at 11:40 Julian is at the top of the stairs. Friday she has two soups and keeps the one Julian brought; Laney tries to tell her
the truth through a door and Lena won't hear it; Margot tells Jack the truth on the stairs and means it. So Theo does the only thing he knows: he puts Jack in a borrowed
shirt and takes him to a house in the hills *not* to meet anyone — and loses him in three minutes. And Jack, for the first time in his life, crosses a room on his own.
For one long night a stranger looks at him like nothing else exists, argues him into the best thing he believes, says what she wants out loud, and leaves on her own timing.
The cost isn't the kiss. It's 6:05 a.m., a roommate in a camel coat asking where he went, and Jack's first lie of omission — and then, at halftime, the invitation that's
been in his mouth for two weeks finally coming out, to Theo, who is too confused to do anything but say yes.

**Register.** Thursday gray and funny-sad (a touch-tank text; "Sure it is"; Gloria takes his apron); the basement warm and electric and unbearable; midnight a farce of

being exactly themselves); the Saab giddy and reckless and, once, devastating; the beach cold and alive; the kiss charged, specific, brief; dawn guilty and
exhilarated; Saturday a comedy of confusion with a Big Ten game on.

**Cadence check.** **Two-hander: delivered** (Thu, ~3,300 words, nobody else until 11:40). **Setpiece:** the Skye meet (a two-person set piece, not an ensemble one);
