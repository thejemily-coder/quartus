**Logline (spoiler-free):** *Christmas comes up early, a piano nobody admits to playing gets played on purpose, and at midnight the town fills up
with vampires.*

**Engine of the episode.** E15 took away the thing Jack was waiting for. E16 is what a decent man does when the fence has nothing left to fence:
the truth, to the girl who did everything right, a week before Thanksgiving. And then the cost, which isn't Becca's tears — it's the
two-hander, the best night they've had since the stairs, ending at 11:40 because Julian is picking her up. Lena is devastated on Wednesday and
radiant on Thursday, and Jack sees neither. The player should see both.

**Register.** Cold open warm and funny (a St. Jude card at a Browns game); Tuesday sweet and unbearable (Becca shining with his mother's
letter); 2 a.m. the knife turns inward (Becca prays at two, like always); Wednesday the hard, kind thing; Wednesday night ten minutes of grace and
a face in a doorway; Thursday the two-hander (piano, Gina, vampires, a man who leaves "for her own good"); midnight vampires; Friday a farce of
soups and a declaration; Saturday the bad night and a letter from Trumbull that arrives a week too late to be advice.

**Cadence check.** **Two-hander: owed and delivered** (Thu, 9:00–11:40 p.m., the Delgado basement; ~4,000 words, nobody else until 11:40).
Setpiece: the Rialto midnight show is a mid-size ensemble room (the floor, forced), not the season's setpiece. Duel: **one, unofficial, inside
the two-hander** (`twilight`; no referee; **the record card is not rewritten** — same policy as E15's `wall`; a separate note card).
