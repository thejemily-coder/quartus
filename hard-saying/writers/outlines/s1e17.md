## 1. SHAPE

**Title:** **"A Good Man"** (retitled from the bible's "Christ the King"; Christ the King is still the Sunday). O'Connor's title, cut in half. Becca
was one and it wasn't the thing; Julian is one and it may not be enough; the Misfit says *there's no real pleasure in life*; the grandmother, at the
end, calls a murderer her child; and Jack — the brief's good man who may not believe he is — spends one night being exactly as good as he wants to be
and wakes up with a secret. Never said on the page.

**Dates:** **Sunday, November 22 (1:00 a.m., the end of Saturday night) – Wednesday, November 25, 2009** (tag ≈8:00 a.m. Wed, in the air). The party
is the night of Sat Nov 21 into Sun Nov 22 (E16 ends Sat 5:45 p.m. with Theo's "What time do you get off?" — this plan **keeps that bridge**: Jack's
Anchor shift ends at 1). **Christ the King** (Sun; Dan 7:13–14; Rev 1:5–8; **John 18:33b–37** — "What is truth?"). **Saints 38, Bucs 7, at Tampa
(10–0)** and **Lions 38, Browns 37** (Stafford's touchdown on the untimed last play) — same Sunday, same 10 a.m. PT window, two TVs at Sully's [both
**verify**]. Mon Nov 23: Hale's paired papers due; 🔒 the Struthers anniversary (silent). Tue Nov 24: **Sts. Andrew Dũng-Lạc and Companions** (the
Vietnamese Martyrs — Kevin's family's feast); **Koenigsegg walks away from buying Saab [verify the date and how it reached US evening radio]**. Wed Nov
25: Thanksgiving break begins; SBA. Moon: a five-day crescent sets mid-evening Saturday — **no moon at all after midnight** (stars; the oil
platforms' lights carry the sea). Dry, cool, clear (climatology only — **no rain beat**). Sunrise Sun ≈6:34 a.m.; first gray ≈5:40.

**Logline (spoiler-free):** *Theo drags Jack to a party in the hills, somebody has Flannery O'Connor exactly backwards, and the whole floor heads home
for Thanksgiving.*

**Engine of the episode.** E16 was a decent man doing the hard thing and paying for it in silence. E17 is the first thing that happens to him that
nobody arranged: he crosses a room. For one long night a stranger looks at him like nothing else exists, argues him into the best thing he believes,
says what she wants out loud, and leaves on her own timing — and the cost is not the kiss, it's the lie of omission at 6:40 a.m. to a roommate who's
lying to him too, and the courtyard Sunday night where Lena asks one question and Jack, for the first time, doesn't quite answer it. Then the week
tips toward home: a fall on the stairs in Youngstown, two plane tickets, two holy cards, a text about an orphaned car.

**Register.** Cold open warm and comic (Theo on the SL's hood at 1 a.m. with a borrowed shirt and a rule); the party glossy, loud, a little lonely;
the den hushed and electric (an argument about grace that is also flirting that is also two people being exactly themselves); the Saab giddy and
reckless and then, once, devastating (a voice dropped); the beach cold and bare and alive; the kiss charged, specific, brief; dawn guilty and
exhilarated at once. Sunday the knife turns from comedy (Dale's Lions) to dread (Gina's text) to grace (Christ the King; a name read aloud). Monday
and Tuesday a sweet scattering — margins, departures, a pie in a box. Wednesday a terminal with four people, three goodbyes, two holy cards and one
phone.

**Cadence check.** **Two-hander: not owed** (E16 had it; **E18's Thanksgiving-night phone call** is the next one, per the bible). Lena still carries
five real beats (Sully's; the Odyssey glass; the courtyard wall; the paired-paper margins; SBA). **Setpiece:** the Skye meet is the episode's
extended set piece — a two-person one, not an ensemble setpiece (the bible's E17 note stands). **Duel:** **one, unofficial, with Skye** (`misfit`;
the record card is Jack–Lena's and is **not** touched; no referee; she scores it herself).

**Length:** **20,000–22,000 words per path** (Skye exception; the meet ≈10,000 of it). 22–25 choices; 8–12 keys; 5–7 cues; 4–5 needle-drops.
