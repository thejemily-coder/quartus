**Logline (spoiler-free):** *A text Jack can't write, a reply that takes all day, a grandmother on the stairs — and coffee at an hour nobody keeps, the night before

**Engine of the episode.** E16 was the first thing that happened to Jack that nobody arranged. E17 is what he does with it, which is mostly wait. He spends a whole morning
of 10–0 and a Lions miracle writing fourteen words on T9; sends them; and then hears nothing through Christ the King, a courtyard where Lena asks one question too many, and

grandmother; and a kiss in a parking lot that is nothing like the first one, slower, on purpose. She leaves. He sits on a curb with a pie for Nonna on his knees and thinks
about her mouth until the sky comes apart from the hills. Three hours later two girls who aren't his give him two holy cards for his grandmother, and the phone buzzes under
them: *window seat. dont blink.*
