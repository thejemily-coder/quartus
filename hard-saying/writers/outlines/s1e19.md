## 1. SHAPE

**Title:** **"Nobody."** "Who're you texting?" "No one." (E17) — and in Book Nine of the *Odyssey*, the name a clever man gives a monster so he can get his men home:
*Outis*, Nobody. Lena has Book Nine for her Greek final on Tuesday (the route), and on the one night of the week she finds him, she gives him the whole of it — and Penelope,
who knew her husband by the one thing only the two of them knew. Also Skye's E18 line, if it plays (*i liked being nobody to you*). Never said in the narration except where a
character says it.

  Washington (10 a.m. PT; E20 opens there).
**Weather:** early December on the Central Coast — cold, clear nights (low 40s; frost inland), sunny 60s afternoons; the moon past full and high after midnight Fri/Sat (waning
gibbous, rising ≈8:30 p.m.) [V]. Sunrise ≈6:55, sunset ≈4:48 [V]. **Time zones:** Skye in LA (same zone) Tue–Thu and Sat–Sun; Lena same zone; Ohio +3.

**Logline (spoiler-free):** *A week of strange hours: a blowout nobody stays for, a speech about thirty thousand, a drive with no destination — and on the one night he's
where he said he'd be, somebody comes to check.*

**Engine of the episode.** Jack Costigan has two lives this week and is very good at both. In one, a girl who is on television drives two hours to kiss him for forty minutes,
calls him at one in the morning to read the President's face, and takes him up the coast at an hour nobody keeps with the top down and Springsteen on the radio — and tells
him, in the moonlight, the first thing she's ever told him that she didn't want to: *I don't take people home.* In the other, he is exactly who everybody thinks he is: the
roommate, the dishwasher, the third believer at the table, the boy on the lawn at 3 a.m. — except that he isn't on the lawn, and everybody notices, and nobody says so. Theo
reads the News Feed aloud like scripture. Laney convenes the alliance in a laundry room. Margot keeps a log. Kevin keeps a seat. And Lena, who is trying with a good man by
daylight, goes looking for Jack in the dark — once, twice, a third time — and on Saturday night, when Julian is in Oakland and Laney is at a formal, she borrows Kevin's car
and drives to the end of the wharf to see if he is where he said he'd be. He is. So they have the night: pie at the Anchor at one, chocolate coins at Walgreens at half past,
the *Odyssey* in Kevin's Odyssey at two, every shoe in Delgado filled for St. Nicholas by four, and an inch in a laundry room at half past four that she closes the only way
she can — by stepping back. **The episode is about two people telling each other everything except the one thing.** He's Nobody to her and Somebody in a Saab; she asks once
where he goes, and then she stops asking. It's the last time they're alone together this year until the night everyone else has gone home.

**Register.** Warmth first and comedy all through (Theo's News Feed as liturgy; Julian ringing the bell; GTL; Theo covering for a man in "the shower" while the building is
evacuated; the alliance discovered among the dryers; Walgreens at 1:35 a.m.; the St. Nicholas raid). Heat in two steps (s03, s11), each with character in it. The knife in
small cuts: "You don't work Mondays"; a text that arrives mid-call; the empty space on the lawn; "I don't take people home"; "Where do you go?"; Penelope and the bed;
"Don't make me be the one who wrecks something, Jack"; and the last text of the episode, where he does to Skye what he's been doing to Lena all week. **Darkly funny while
sad** (brief): every move he makes is a volley he doesn't know he's firing.

**Cadence check.** **Two-hander with Lena: YES — s15–s21 (≈6,000), the Romance Writer's chunk; it carries restraint #2** (moved from the bible's E20). **Ensemble setpiece:** no
full setpiece (E18's Glenwood was one; E21's Newman party is next) — two small ensemble rooms (the Fishbowl for the speech; the Fishbowl party → the lawn). **Duel:** `outis`
(unofficial, the Odyssey, three scenes). **Skye:** three on-page meetings by phone or in person (s03, s05, s10–s11) plus the week's thread.
