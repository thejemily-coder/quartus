## 1. SHAPE

**Title:** **"Nobody."** "Who're you texting?" "No one." (E17) — and in Book Nine of the *Odyssey*, the name a clever man gives a monster so he can get his men home: *Outis*, Nobody. Lena has
Book Nine for her Greek final on Tuesday (the route), and on the one night of the week she finds him, she gives him the whole of it — and Penelope, who knew her husband by the one thing only the
two of them knew. Also Skye's E18 line on one path ([`e18_tv` candor] *I liked being nobody to you*) and Gina's oath ("Nobody. I know."). Never said in the narration except where a character says it.

**Dates:** **Monday, November 30 – Sunday, December 6, 2009 (morning).**

  (10 a.m. PT; E20 opens there). Maria is at Trumbull this morning ("Sunday week," E18) — no line required.
**Weather:** dry week (the first storm is Mon Dec 7 [V] — E20's); cold clear nights (low 40s; high 30s inland), sunny 60s afternoons; the moon full Tue night, waning gibbous and high after midnight
Fri/Sat; sunrise ≈6:50, sunset ≈4:48. **Time zones:** Skye in LA Tue–Thu and Sat–Sun; Lena same zone; Ohio +3.

**Logline (spoiler-free):** *A week of strange hours: a blowout nobody stays for, a speech about thirty thousand, a drive with no destination — and on the one night he's where he said he'd be,
somebody comes to check.*

**Engine of the episode.** Jack Costigan has two lives this week and is very good at both. In one, a girl who is on television drives two hours to kiss him for forty minutes, calls him at one
in the morning to read the President's face, sends him at five a.m. a picture of herself in a makeup chair that costs a quarter and that he has to carry into a stairwell to see, and takes him up
the coast at an hour nobody keeps with the top down and Springsteen on the radio — and tells him, in the moonlight, the first thing she's ever told him that she didn't want to: *I don't take people
home.* In the other, he is exactly who everybody thinks he is: the roommate, the dishwasher, the third believer at the table, the boy on the lawn at 3 a.m. — except that he isn't on the lawn, and
everybody notices, and nobody says so. Theo reads the News Feed aloud like scripture and notices how fast a phone can go into a pocket. Laney convenes the alliance in a laundry room. Margot keeps
a log. Kevin keeps a seat. And Lena, who is trying with a good man by daylight, goes looking for Jack in the dark — once, twice, a third time — and on Saturday night, when Julian is in Oakland and
Laney is at a formal, she borrows Kevin's car and drives to the end of the wharf to see if he is where he said he'd be. He is. So they have the night: pie at the Anchor at one, chocolate coins at
Walgreens at half past, the *Odyssey* in Kevin's Odyssey at two, every shoe in Delgado filled for St. Nicholas by four, and an inch in a laundry room at half past four that she closes the only
way she can — by stepping back, while something he will not look at buzzes in his jacket against her knee. **The episode is about two people telling each other everything except the one
thing.** He's Nobody to her and Somebody in a Saab; she asks once where he goes, and then she stops asking. It's the last time they're alone together this year until the night everyone else has
gone home.

**Register.** Warmth first and comedy all through (Theo's News Feed as liturgy; Julian ringing the bell; a picture message that won't load in 412; the Situation Room; Theo covering for a man in "the shower"
while the building is evacuated; the alliance among the dryers; Walgreens at 1:35 a.m.; the St. Nicholas raid). Heat in two steps (s03, s11) and two pictures, each with character in it. The knife in
small cuts: "You don't work Mondays"; a text that arrives mid-call; the empty space on the lawn; "I don't take people home"; "a hand of cards"; "Where do you go?"; Penelope and the bed; "Don't
make me be the one who wrecks something, Jack"; and the last text of the episode, where he does to Skye what he's been doing to Lena all week. **Darkly funny while sad** (brief).

**Cadence check.** **Two-hander with Lena: YES — s15–s21 (≈6,000), the Romance Writer's chunk; it carries restraint #2.** **Ensemble setpiece:** none (E18's Glenwood was one; E21's Newman party is
next) — two small ensemble rooms (the Fishbowl for the speech; the Fishbowl party → the lawn). **Duel:** `outis` (unofficial, three scenes). **Skye:** three on-page meetings by phone or in person
