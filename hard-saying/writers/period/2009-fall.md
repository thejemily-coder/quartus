For the writers' room. Prepared by the Period Researcher. Scope: move-in Saturday, Aug 29, 2009 through the last Sunday before winter break (Dec 20, 2009).

**Verification legend.** `[V]` = confirmed by web search during preparation. Unmarked = well-established fact I am confident of from general knowledge. `UNVERIFIED` = could not be confirmed; treat as flavor, do not build a plot beat on it without checking. Where a detail is uncertain I say so inline.

**Three things the writers must not miss**
1. **Saturday, Aug 29, 2009 is the 4th anniversary of Hurricane Katrina's landfall (Aug 29, 2005).** It is also the day of Ted Kennedy's Catholic funeral Mass in Boston, and it is the pilot's move-in day / party night. Lena is from New Orleans. See Sec. 6.
2. **The Sunday of the Mass-after-the-party (Aug 30, 2009) is the 22nd Sunday in Ordinary Time, Gospel Mark 7:1-8, 14-15, 21-23** [V by lectionary structure; cross-checked with the Year B sequence]. And the Sunday *before* (Aug 23) the Gospel was **John 6:60-69: "This is a hard saying; who can listen to it?"**, the game's title verse. See Sec. 1.
3. **The Mass is still the old English translation.** "And also with you." The new translation ("And with your spirit") begins **Nov 27, 2011** (First Sunday of Advent) [V]. See Sec. 5.

## 1. CALENDAR

### 1.1 Liturgical frame
- Sundays through Nov 22, 2009 = **Year B** (Mark). Advent 2009 (Nov 29) begins **Year C** (Luke). Weekday cycle in 2009 = **Year I** (odd year).
- Aug 30 count: Trinity Sunday June 7; Corpus Christi (US, moved to Sunday) June 14; so June 21 = 12th, Aug 30 = **22nd Sunday in Ordinary Time**.
- Easter 2009 was April 12; Pentecost May 31. Easter 2010 is April 4; Ash Wednesday 2010 is Feb 17; Mardi Gras Feb 16, 2010.
- Assumption (Aug 15, 2009) fell on a Saturday; in the US the obligation is lifted when it falls on a Saturday or Monday. Not relevant to the pilot except as color.
- **Year for Priests** (declared by Benedict XVI) runs June 19, 2009 – June 11, 2010. Priests everywhere reference the Curé of Ars (St. John Vianney). Good for Fr. Osei.
- Mass texts: 1973/1998 ICEL translation (see Sec. 5).
