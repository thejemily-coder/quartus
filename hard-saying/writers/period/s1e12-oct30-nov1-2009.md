## 0. CORRECTIONS AND TRAPS (read first)
1. **There is no "second two o'clock."** The bible says Jack finds Theo "at the wall at the second two o'clock." At 2:00 a.m. PDT clocks go back to **1:00 a.m. PST**, so the hour that repeats is **1:00-1:59**; 2:00 a.m. happens once (as PST). Fix: "the second one o'clock" / "the second 1:30" / "the second time it was 1:59." The "hour that happens twice" title idea stands. (Bible: they leave the party at 12:55; the second 1:00 arrives about an hour after the first.)
