### 1.7 Places
- **CSU Marisol**, Central Coast of California. Classes begin Mon Aug 31, 2009; Labor Day Sept 7; Thanksgiving break
  Nov 25–29; finals Dec 14–18.
- **Delgado Hall** (Anacapa complex, on the Lagoon): 5 floors, co-ed by room. Ground: lobby, front desk, the Fishbowl
  lounge. Basement: laundry. 2nd: 217 Lena & Laney; 204 Rafi. 3rd: 305 Margot. 4th: 412 Jack & Theo; 420 Kevin & Doug;
  401 Priya (RA); 4th-floor lounge with a whiteboard. 5th: roof door "ALARM WILL SOUND" (it won't). East stairwell: best
  cell reception; windows open six inches.
- **Hollis Hall** (1920s Spanish Revival; Philosophy/Classics): Hale's office 314; seminar room 302 (PHIL 9H); section room
  108 (basement).
- **Pacifica Library** ("the Brick"), **the Commons Lawn**, **Ferrer Tower**, **the Grove** (eucalyptus; monarchs in
  winter), **the Lagoon**, **Campus Point**, **the Marine Lab**, **the UCen** (*Marisol Tide* newsroom).
- **Las Olas** (student town west of the Grove): **Seacliff Road** (Greek row: Pi Lambda Chi, Kappa Alpha Delta, Sigma Rho,
  Alpha Tau Kappa; then surf rentals); **the Lighthouse** (last house on Seacliff before the Point; back porch faces the
  Point; gap in the back fence; a bluff path); **Del Playa Lane**; **Shipwreck Cove** (bonfire beach; wooden stairs; old
  schooner ribs).
- **Marisol Point**: headland, ~70-ft bluff, the decommissioned Point Marisol Light (1890s; dark since 1967), a low stone
  wall, a small dirt lot; a white wooden memorial cross at the wall.
- **St. Brendan the Navigator Newman Center** (across Ocean Road, edge of the Grove): 1962 A-frame, ceiling like an
  overturned ship's hull, blue glass, driftwood crucifix, Marian shrine with votive rack and a small brass memorial plate;
  courtyard with an olive tree and a broken sprinkler. Masses: Sat 5:00 p.m.; Sun 9:30, 11:30, 5:00, 9:00 p.m.; weekdays
  12:10 p.m. Confession: Sat 3:30–4:45; Wed 8:00 p.m.
- **Town** (3 mi east on Mission St.): the Anchor (24-hr diner on the harbor wharf; red vinyl, pie case, jukebox); Café
  Bastille (Thursday open mic); the Wharf Rat (dive bar); Sully's (sports bar); Lupe's (taco truck, Seacliff, 10 p.m.–3
  a.m.); Driftwood Books (cats: Boethius, Mrs. Danvers); Giorgio's (pizza); Old Mission Nuestra Señora del Mar; Marisol PD
  on Anacapa Street; Walgreens on Mission Street; the harbor (sea lions).
- Nearest airport: Santa Barbara (SBA), ~55 min south. Amtrak Pacific Surfliner/Coast Starlight on the bluff; a freight
  horn around 2:10 a.m.
