# THE LIE WE TOLD — Series Canon (player-approved, spoiler-free)

This file records every decision the player (the "network") made in development. It is binding on the whole writers' room. Nothing here is secret; secrets live only in `SPOILERS-showrunner-only/`.

## Format
- Interactive prestige-TV college drama in prose. Played in a published HTML page, one episode at a time.
- **Bespoke episodes.** No pre-built decision tree. After the player finishes an episode, the room reads the exact save (every choice, every flag) and writes the next episode around it. Small choices can grow into whole subplots later. Episodes branch internally for immediate consequences. Choices must be genuinely meaningful — never illusory, never funnelled back to an identical outcome without leaving a mark.
- **Seasons are story arcs, not academic years.** Each semester contains at least two seasons (including breaks); each summer at least one. Episode counts vary with story needs. Season 1 begins Saturday, August 27, 2005 (freshman move-in).
- **Endgame:** four college years, then open-ended post-grad seasons (their twenties: D.C., New York, campaigns). The showrunner plans a college-era finale that could stand as an ending.
- **Episode length:** a prestige hour — 45–60 minutes of reading, ~8,000–12,000 words along any single path, 12–18 choices along a path. Cold open, 4–5 acts, tag.
- **POV:** close third person on Cal throughout (past tense). Occasional **playable Del cutaways** in close third on Del — the player makes her choices; Cal doesn't know what happened. Del scenes get a distinct warm-gold visual grade. Rare read-only cutaways to other characters are allowed (dramatic irony).
- **Consequences are hidden.** No meters, no stats. The player learns what choices meant from how people treat Cal. Occasional subtle cue ("Del will remember that.") is allowed but sparing.
- **Choice types:** dialogue tone (charm/honesty/cruelty/deflection), action/where-to-go (the player can miss scenes entirely), moral dilemmas (no right answer). Choices inside intimate scenes are allowed: emotional AND physical-direction choices.
- **No timed choices. No free-text input.**
- **Rewind:** player may undo the most recent choice within an episode; choices lock when the episode ends.
- **Flourishes:** "Previously On" recap at the start of each episode; "Next Time On" teaser (shown on the title screen once the next episode is published); episode titles taken from a lyric or song title released on or before the episode's in-world date; needle-drop music cues in text (song, artist, where it's playing) and an end-of-episode tracklist; Cal's/Del's burned mix CDs (player picks period-accurate tracks; the recipient reacts in later episodes); Del's disposable-camera Polaroid snapshots, collected into a scrapbook (some become clues); a cryptic end card of 3–4 choices that will echo.
- **Period extras (hybrid look):** in-world artifacts rendered authentically — AIM chats & away messages, flip-phone texts (T9-era), early Facebook walls/pokes (2005: college-only; `thefacebook` → Facebook in Aug–Sept 2005), handwritten notes, voicemails. These EVOLVE with the chronology (Facebook News Feed Sept 2006, open registration Sept 2006, iPhone June 2007, Twitter, etc.).
- **Season-level check-ins** with the player happen at season boundaries (spoiler-free AskUserQuestion rounds about tone/focus).

## Tone
Euphoria-led, Gilmore heart. Raw, sensory, dangerous, with real consequences; warmed by fast, witty, literate banter and found-family tenderness. Greek and Entourage supply the social machinery and fun. Maximalist and unflinching. Influences: Gilmore Girls, Euphoria, Entourage, Greek.

## Content
- Frank, mature, explicit. Sex scenes are fully explicit and detailed, but always character- and emotion-driven: what it means to them, their history, power dynamics, what each is hiding, how it changes. Their sexual relationship evolves as its own storyline; no two scenes read alike. Frequent is fine; spectacle for its own sake is not.
- Fully depictable: drugs & addiction (2005 party culture — coke, pills, molly, alcohol), violence (fights, Southie street world, hazing), mental health (era-honest, pre-vocabulary), betrayal.
- **Every character is 18 or older from the first scene.** No exceptions, ever, including backstory sexual content.
- Room rule: sexual violence may exist in backstory or in the mystery but is never depicted explicitly on-page and never eroticized.

## HARD LINES (never cross)
1. **Cal and Del never cheat on each other and never sleep with anyone else — not even while broken up.** They make each other jealous to provoke a response; they fight; they can split; they never mean it. They are each other's anchor no matter how much they storm around it. They are drawn irresistibly to each other.
2. **Cal's twins (Siobhan and Patrick) never die.** They can be in danger.
3. Cal and Del sleep together on Night One (the pilot). That is the premise.

## Story engines (all active)
Del's lie · Cal's secrets · the world vs. them (class, family, the society, Graham, Kit) · their own fire (two arsonists who dare and escalate). Plus: a campus mystery (writers decide everything; the player knows only that there is one), power & status (secret society, Greek row, final clubs, student government/debate), family secrets (both leads), ambition & identity. A **hidden antagonist** works against Cal and/or Del from the shadows.

Because the romance is anchored, it cannot be the only engine. If they are together, happy and stable early, the other engines must pull hard. Complications early; organic fights throughout.

## Setting
- **Hollis University**, founded 1791, a fictional old-money elite private university in a small college town on the Connecticut River valley (Massachusetts/New Hampshire/Vermont border region feel — brick and ivy, brutal winters, autumn color, a river, a townie bar; a train to New York ~2+ hours; ~90 minutes from Boston/Southie by car; Peter Pan bus).
- Power structures: a centuries-old **secret senior society** with a windowless "tomb" that taps juniors; a **dominant Greek row** (two rival top fraternities, two rival top sororities); **student government / debate / honor council**; **final clubs / eating clubs** (private, older money, more exclusive than Greek).
- ~70% campus; regular trips to Southie, New York, the Cape/Nantucket, spring break, summer interludes.
- The student body mostly mirrors a 2005 elite school (overwhelmingly white and wealthy); class is the main fault line.

## Real history the show tracks
Hurricane Katrina (landfall Aug 29, 2005 — hits in Episode 2; Rafe can't reach his New Orleans family; the group comes together around him) · the Iraq War (someone from Southie may enlist; campus protests; Kit's family is pro-war) · tech shift (Facebook opens up 2006, iPhone 2007, Twitter) · the 2008 election & financial crash (senior year; Teddy's father's firm) · culture & music (Killers/Bloc Party → MGMT/Vampire Weekend/Lil Wayne; Red Sox, Patriots, Celtics matter in Southie — the Sox won the Series in 2004).

## THE LEADS

### Callum "Cal" Reardon — protagonist
- 18. From **South Boston (Southie)**. Irish-Catholic, working class. Full-ride scholarship to Hollis.
- **Look:** 6'0", lean and wiry from summer construction work; black hair always needing a cut; pale gray-blue eyes; crooked smile; a small scar through one eyebrow from a story he tells differently every time; thrift-store clothes he wears well.
- **Temperament (starting):** charming and reckless. Talks his way into rooms and trouble alike. Impulsive, warm, with a self-destructive streak he doesn't see. Southie wit. (Player choices shape him over time.)
- **Ambition:** law and politics. (Debate, student government, honor council are his natural arenas.)
- **Money:** works nights as a barback at the townie bar (owner is a gruff, warm surrogate-father figure with a long memory of Hollis kids). Sends money home every week.
- **Greek life:** Cal stays OUT of Greek life — an independent outsider who mocks and envies it.
- **Baggage (seeds; truths are secret):** absent father (mom has never told him the whole story) · twin siblings at home he half-raised · a summer (2005) he won't discuss · his full ride is funded by a named private donor whose interest in him isn't entirely clear.
- **Family:**
  - **Patty Reardon**, 38, his mother. Bartender at a Southie pub. Young, glamorous in a battered way, more friend than mother. Unreliable, beloved; Cal worries she'll fall apart while he's gone. Had Cal at 20.
  - **Siobhan and Patrick Reardon**, 12, twins. Cal is basically their second parent. Leaving them is a guilt he carries every day. (They never die.)
- **Declan "Deck" Fahey**, 19, Cal's best friend since first communion. Works construction with his uncle; runs small jobs for some neighborhood guys. 5'10", stocky, red-faced, huge laugh. Loyal to the bone, a little jealous. The only person who knows what happened "that summer."

### Delphine "Del" Ashcombe — female lead
- Presents as 18, a freshman. **The self-made shapeshifter**: she came from nothing (true origin decided by the showrunner and hidden from the player) and has reinvented herself so completely that everyone believes she's old money. Cal is the one person who can eventually see through it — which makes him dangerous and irresistible to her. Her name/identity may itself be part of the costume.
- **Look:** 5'6", sun-streaked blonde, tanned (the story is "Nantucket summers"), light amber eyes, a long dancer's body. Looks like a magazine ad; talks like a sailor.
- **Surface temperament:** warm and wild. Big-hearted and impulsive, cries at sad songs and fistfights at bars. Her volatility is honest; it's her history that's fake.
- **Ambition:** she doesn't know. She's been someone else so long she has no idea what she wants. Finding out is her arc.
- **Roommate:** Caroline "Caro" Pell (random assignment).
- **Romantic complication:** Graham Ashford (below) — what she could secure if she chose.

### The romance
"Two arsonists." Both reckless, both escalate. They dare each other, start fires, are drawn to the same danger. The heat is a mutual addiction; so is the damage. Volatile, deeply intense, begins Night One, carries them through four years and beyond. They come of age together and learn who they are.

**Pilot cold open:** Saturday, August 27, 2005, move-in day. Cal gets off a Peter Pan bus with a duffel and a borrowed suitcase while Teddy's parents unload a Range Rover. That night, **caught in the same lie**: at a party neither was invited to (frat, final club or society-adjacent), both are bluffing their way through. Each catches the other mid-con. They make a pact of silence that turns into the most electric night of their lives — and ends in bed.

## THE CORE FIVE

### Theodore "Teddy" Vanderhoef IV — Cal's roommate (legacy prince; closeted)
18, Greenwich, CT. Fourth-generation Hollis legacy; father a Wall Street titan; grandfather's name is on the boathouse. 6'1", golden-brown hair, blue eyes, rower's build, easy laugh. Generous to a fault, funny, loyal. Pledging his father's fraternity. Closeted and terrified; has a hometown "girlfriend" who knows. Cal is the first person he's ever shared a room with. Adopts Cal; Cal's opposite and best friend. Entourage "Vince" energy — access to every door.

### Caroline "Caro" Pell — Del's best friend & roommate (true heiress)
18, Charleston, SC, old shipping money. 5'5", chestnut hair in a ribbon, big brown eyes, inherited pearls. Gracious, sheltered, secretly the smartest person in the room — pre-med, chemistry prodigy. Family expects her to pledge her mother's sorority and marry well. Adores Del as the wild sister she never had. Believes Del's story completely. Del's lie would destroy her.

### Katherine "Kit" Brennan — the striver rival
18, Columbus, OH. Father: Republican state senator. Mother: former beauty queen turned campaign manager. 5'6", sleek dark-auburn bob, sharp hazel eyes, always in a blazer. Ruthless debater, genuinely brilliant, funny in a dry, murderous way, lonely. Catholic like Cal — country-club parish. Clocks Cal on day one as her only real competition. Cal's main romantic complication: a slow-burn charged "what if" — the partner who'd help him win. (Never consummated with Cal — hard line 1.)

### Rafe Delacroix-Moore — the party chaos agent
19, a second-year freshman (his "gap year" was rehab). New Orleans and Manhattan money. 5'10", wiry, dark shaggy hair, chipped nail polish, Strokes look. Hysterically funny, a dealer's address book, an inexplicable soft spot for Cal. His family is in New Orleans when Katrina hits.

### Henry Oduya-Clarke — the quiet watcher
18, Hartford. Hollis's first legacy whose father was a groundskeeper there. 5'9", slight, wire glasses, always with a notebook. Shy, observant; knows the campus's history better than the archivists. Underestimated; secretly knows more than anyone about the campus mystery.

## RECURRING
- **Julian Thorne**, 21, junior, the freshman dorm's RA. Poli-sci star, debate captain, rumored to be tapped by the secret society. 6'2", elegant, dark-haired, gray suits. Charismatic and cynical; sees Cal as a project. Motives a mystery. Says frats are beneath real power.
- **Graham Ashford**, 21, senior, president of the top fraternity. Old money, impossibly handsome, rumored to be close to whatever happened before the freshmen arrived. Not a villain, but dangerous. Del's main complication.
- **Designed by the writers' room (player meets them fresh):** the townie bar and its owner; a charismatic professor (former White House speechwriter, political theory; Cal's intellectual hero; secrets of his own); the Dean of Students (the institution's polished fixer, hiding everything about what happened); Cal's mystery donor; Graham's deeper details; Del's real history; the hidden antagonist; the campus mystery.
