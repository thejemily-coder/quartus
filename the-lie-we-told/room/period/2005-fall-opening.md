## 1. HURRICANE KATRINA, day by day (ties to Ep 2; Rafe's family in New Orleans)
- **Tue Aug 23:** Tropical Depression Twelve forms near the Bahamas. [K]
- **Wed Aug 24:** Becomes Tropical Storm Katrina. [K]
- **Thu Aug 25:** Landfall in South Florida (Miami-Dade/Broward) as a Cat 1, evening; flooding, about a dozen deaths in FL. Crosses into the Gulf Aug 26. [K]
- **Fri Aug 26:** Louisiana and Mississippi governors declare states of emergency. Forecast track shifts west toward the Louisiana/Mississippi coast. [K]
- **SAT AUG 27 (PILOT DAY):** Katrina is a major hurricane (Cat 3, intensifying) in the Gulf. Cable news and The Weather Channel carry the cone: it "could" hit New Orleans. Mayor Ray Nagin calls a voluntary evacuation (Saturday evening); Bush approves an emergency declaration for Louisiana. In a Northeast dorm lobby this is a TV in the background, "that big storm in the Gulf", not yet a personal event. Rafe's family would be debating whether to leave: many wealthy New Orleanians left Saturday; many others could not or would not. [K]
- **Sun Aug 28:** Katrina reaches Cat 5 (about 160-175 mph, peak intensity). NHC warns of catastrophic flooding. Nagin orders the first-ever MANDATORY evacuation of New Orleans (morning). Contraflow on the interstates, hours-long jams. Superdome opens as a "refuge of last resort" (about 10,000 by night, later 25,000+). Bush joins a briefing video-conference with FEMA's Michael Brown and NHC's Max Mayfield. Same night: MTV VMAs in Miami (see Music). [K]
- **MON AUG 29 (EPISODE 2 TERRITORY):** Landfall about 6:10 a.m. CDT near Buras, LA, as a strong Cat 3 (about 125 mph); second landfall near the LA/MS border about 10 a.m. The Superdome roof is torn. New Orleans appears to have "dodged the bullet" in the first hours of coverage; then the levees fail: 17th Street Canal, London Avenue Canal, Industrial Canal (Lower Ninth Ward, St. Bernard). Mississippi Gulf Coast (Biloxi, Gulfport, Waveland, Bay St. Louis) is devastated by storm surge. [K]
- **Tue Aug 30:** About 80 percent of New Orleans flooded; rooftop rescues, people on I-10 overpasses; looting reports; Superdome conditions worsen (no AC, toilets failing). Bush cuts his Crawford vacation short, flies back. [K]
- **Wed Aug 31:** Bush's Air Force One flyover (the famous window photo). Evacuation of Superdome to Houston Astrodome begins (Aug 31/Sept 1). Convention Center crowds grow. Reports and rumors of violence, many later disproved or exaggerated. [K]
- **Thu Sept 1:** Convention Center holds roughly 20,000 with no food, water, or police. Nagin's WWL radio interview ("get off your asses and do something"). [K]
- **FRI SEPT 2:** National Guard convoys finally reach the Convention Center. Bush tours Mobile and New Orleans; "Brownie, you're doing a heck of a job." Evening: NBC's "A Concert for Hurricane Relief"; Kanye West goes off-script beside Mike Myers: "George Bush doesn't care about black people." NBC cut to Chris Tucker. [V: the date and the quote's gist were confirmed; exact wording is disputed in reports.]
- **Sat Sept 3:** Evacuations continue; Chief Justice Rehnquist dies that evening. [K]
- **Sun Sept 4 - Mon Sept 5 (Labor Day):** Bodies begin to be recovered visibly; Michael Brown loses his on-scene role Sept 9. Coverage turns to the 'blame' phase. Bush names Roberts as nominee for Chief Justice on Sept 5. [K]
- **Later:** Eventual deaths roughly 1,800+ (about 1,830 commonly cited); one of the costliest US disasters. Hurricane Rita threatens the same region Sept 24. [K]
- **COMMUNICATIONS (key for Rafe):** Landlines and switching centers flooded or lost power; reports of more than 3 million phone lines down across the Gulf [?: figure recalled, verify]. Cell towers down or without power or backhaul. Calls into the 504 area code (New Orleans) mostly returned fast-busy or "all circuits busy"; **text messages sometimes got through when voice did not**; people with 'out-of-area' relatives used a relative in another state as a message relay; people pinned notes to boards; message boards and early message-board "I'm okay" pages (Craigslist, Katrina people-finders, Red Cross Safe and Well); Cingular/Sprint/Nextel/Verizon coverage spotty. Family could go four or five days with no word. Good beats: Rafe refreshing a phone with no bars; texting 504 numbers that deliver hours late; a stranger's out-of-state 'relay' relative; calling an uncle's Manhattan number to ask whether anyone has heard. [K/?]
- **TV texture:** CNN (Anderson Cooper's anger, Aaron Brown), Fox (Shepard Smith at the overpass), MSNBC, Weather Channel (Cantore), local WWL-TV and WDSU feeds. People watched at student-center TVs, dorm common rooms. Rumor-heavy first week. [K]
