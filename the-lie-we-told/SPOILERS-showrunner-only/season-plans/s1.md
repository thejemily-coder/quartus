# SEASON ONE — "FIRST LIGHT" (SHOWRUNNER ONLY)

**In-world span:** Saturday, August 27, 2005 (freshman move-in) → Monday, October 10, 2005 (Columbus Day; the last day of Hollis fall break).
**Episodes:** 8 (flexible 7–9 by player choices; E1, E2 and E8 are fixed tentpoles).
**Bible:** `../series-bible.md` governs. Hard lines govern everything.

## LOGLINE
On their first night at Hollis, two con artists swear "we were never here." For six weeks they try to keep that promise — to each other, to a campus still hiding a dead girl, and to a stranger who saw them at the river and has started sending Cal photographs.

## SEASON ENGINES
- **A — The Pact.** Cal and Del: one night that refuses to stay one night. A secret relationship (her rule) between a boy who reads lies for a living and a girl made of them. Anchored: they're in love by E4 whether they admit it or not. The season's romantic question isn't *will they* but *how much will they let the other see.*
- **B — Jo.** The shrine, the oar blade, the figure, the Race, Julian. The campus is grieving a girl it pretends it never met.
- **C — Ambition.** HUM 117 (Sloane's "The Uses of Lying"), the Disputation, Kit, the Haverford Scholars, Owen. Cal discovers he's very, very good at the thing he's best at, which is talking.
- **D — Home.** Katrina (Rafe); Southie (Patty, Deck, the twins, Coughlin); Del's secret Gulf family (seen only at an angle).
- **E — The friends.** Teddy's rush and closet; Caro's mother and the Graham match; Rafe's grief and drugs; Henry's knowing silence.
- **Antagonist track.** Three envelopes to Cal (E1, E5, E8) and one to Del (E7). Owen is Cal's favorite upperclassman all season.

## FIXED BEATS THIS SEASON (cannot be skipped by choices)
1. E1: the pact, the dam sighting, Night One in bed, the "Theodore?" envelope.
2. E2: Katrina; Del vanishes ~48 hours; the group forms around Rafe; Cal starts at the Race.
3. E3: first HUM 117 session; Cal meets Owen properly (Haverford lunch); "Haverford" appears in Sloane's full name on the syllabus.
4. E5: second envelope (Gold Street back porch).
5. E6: Patty arrives at Family Weekend, sees Sloane, leaves. Siobhan: "Which one's Professor Sloane?"
6. E7: Julian at the shrine ("Josette"). Del's envelope (Cal and Kit).
7. E8: Graham recognizes Del. The third envelope: Ricky Dolan's death notice.

## PILOT REQUIREMENTS (S1E1 "Somebody Told Me")

**Date/time span:** Sat Aug 27, 2005, 6:50 a.m. → Sun Aug 28, ~7:00 a.m. Weather: hot, hazy, 86°F, thunderheads that never break; a big moon after midnight. Katrina is a Category 3 in the Gulf; it's on TVs in the background all day and no one at Hollis is paying attention yet.

- **Henry & Joseph Clarke** — Henry (wire glasses, notebook) and his father in a Hollis-green groundskeeper's shirt carrying a mini-fridge into Sutter. Cal helps. Henry, unprompted: "The river's that way. They fenced the dam two weeks ago. A girl drowned." Joseph: "Henry." Henry stops.

### The party — whose and why
- **The Corinthian Club's "First Light,"** 14 Prospect Street. Hosted by **Archie Whitby**, Corinthian president. Every year on the Saturday of move-in. Engraved, **non-transferable** cards to legacy freshman men and to members' guests; women attend only as members' guests. Black tie optional; most wear blazers. A jazz trio on the terrace ("Fly Me to the Moon," "Night and Day"), the walled garden lit with hurricane lanterns, the boxwood parterre, the library, the wine cellar. Lethe-adjacent: half the senior members are Lethe. The first night of the year for the people who run Hollis.
- **Why Cal crashes:** Julian's dare + ambition (he wants to see the room) + the reckless streak. Teddy is invited (legacy) but is "going to Sig Rho with Dad's friends" — actually (secret) driving to Northampton. At 8:30 p.m., Teddy, laughing, presses his engraved card into Cal's hand: "Go be me. I dare you. Just don't fuck anyone I'm related to." Cal goes as **Theodore Vanderhoef IV** in his one jacket (his dead grandfather's navy blazer, sleeves an inch short) and Teddy's tie (Sig Rho navy-and-white stripe, which marks him to the knowing as a legacy).
- **Cal's lie:** He is Teddy Vanderhoef of Greenwich. Under it, the other lie: the scar is from hockey.
- **Why Del crashes:** She must test her disguise against **Graham Ashford**, the one person on campus who might have seen "the before" (she served at his family's Nantucket party, Aug 2004), and she wants to do it on her terms — at night, in a dress, in a crowd, before he sees her in daylight. And: you only get one first night, and she wants the people who run Hollis to see Delphine Ashcombe in it. And the thrill.
- **How Del gets in:** Thursday she took a server shift with **Pioneer Valley Catering** under the name **"Dee"** (PVC is short-staffed — "since the Lavoie girl"). Saturday at 6 p.m. she's in black pants and a white shirt in the Corinthian kitchen. Captain **Bev Szalinski** (50s, smoker's laugh) hands her a spare apron from the van — the name tape says **JO**. At ~10:30 she slips into a second-floor linen closet off the service corridor and comes out in Nell's 1961 **green silk Balenciaga** (altered), Nell's pearls, the Cartier watch, hair down. She passes as a guest: if asked, she's "Archie's cousin's friend" (Archie is too drunk to know his cousins).
- **Del's lie:** She is a guest. Under it, everything.

### How each catches the other
1. **Act Two, the terrace:** Cal, as Teddy, takes a glass from a server and says, looking her in the eye, **"Thanks, Jo."** (He read the apron.) She freezes — for a reason he can't know. Nobody else at the party has looked at a server all night. She clocks: *he looked at me. Nobody here looks at the staff.* And she clocks that when Archie shouted "Vanderhoef!" across the terrace, it took him a full second to turn.
2. **Act Three, the service corridor (10:40 p.m.):** Cal ducks into the service corridor to escape Theodore III's old roommate asking about Greenwich (a near-catch: "How's your mother's hip?"). The linen-closet door opens: the server from the terrace, now in green silk, zipping her dress. Beat. Both: *oh.* She: "Zip me or leave." He zips her. He: "You're Jo." She: "And you're Teddy Vanderhoef." He: "How'd you know I'm not?" She (the list of tells, delicious, fast): you thanked the help; you hold your glass by the bowl; Teddy Vanderhoef's tie is on a boy whose shoes were resoled in a basement; and "you answer to Teddy like it's a dog you're walking." He: the apron; "you came out of a closet in a dress that costs more than my mother's car"; "nobody who belongs here knows where the linen closet is."
3. **Mutual rescue (immediately):** Bev comes looking for "Dee"; Cal, in his best Teddy, sends Bev to fetch "my father's coat from the car." Then Archie barrels up and calls Cal "Vanderhoef" and asks about Greenwich; Del, as a guest, hooks Cal's arm: "Teddy, darling, you *promised* you'd introduce me to your mother," and walks him away.

### The river (Act Four)

They kiss against the fence — the first kiss (unless the dares produced an earlier one; writers' choice).
- **The figure, ~1:50 a.m.:** headlights off, a boxy car rolls into the pull-off and stops. Cal and Del drop behind the bushes by the fence. A man in a **Corinthian blazer** (bottle green, brass buttons — they wore them all night) walks to the shrine with a penlight, reads the oar blade, and stands very still. He hums a few bars — **Cal knows the tune: "The Parting Glass." They played it at Ricky Dolan's wake in July.** (One line of Cal's interiority; no explanation of Ricky.) The man pulls the oar blade off the fence and throws it, hard, into the mill race; it goes over the Ladder and is gone. He stands a long time. The penlight swings once across the bushes — across them? — and away. He drives off with no lights until the bridge. They never see his face. (Writers: height, build, the blazer, the hum, the car — nothing more. It is Owen. Do not signal it.)
- **The lie we told:** Shaking, crouched in the bushes: Del: "We weren't here." Cal: "We were never here." "Not at the party. Not at the river." "Not anywhere." It's a joke and an oath. They don't know they've just sworn to protect a killer. (`s1e1_pact_river`.) Choice for Cal: agree / say "we should tell someone" (Del: "Tell them what? That Teddy Vanderhoef and a waitress named Jo saw a man throw away a sorry note?" → he agrees anyway; flag `s1e1_cal_wanted_to_report`).
