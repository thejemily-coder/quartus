## NOTES AFTER S1E1 (showrunner)
- Every fixed pilot beat landed: the catch, the pact (+rule two), the '85 Krug, Graham's "Have we met?", the shrine and blade, the figure (no face; hum; boxy wagon; lights at the bridge), "We were never here," Night One, the 228 call, both Polaroids, Teddy at 6:45, the envelope.
- Envelope timing locked: Del left (~5:10) before Owen slid it under the door (after first light). Cal heard the stairwell door twice. Del does **not** know the envelope exists. Use that in E2: Cal must decide whether to tell her.
- The figure scene was tightened so Cal does not consciously connect the hum to Owen. The hum is fair-play for the player only.
- Act structure as aired: COLD OPEN (bus/curb) · ACT ONE (move-in → card) · ACT TWO (Corinthian → Sloane's look) · ACT THREE (catch → over the wall, "So which way is it?") · ACT FOUR (diner → river → "we were never here") · ACT FIVE (Sutter 304) · TAG (depot, Polaroid, envelope).
- Real path length ≈ 13,500–14,400 words (build's "path words" overcounts parallel `{if}` blocks). Long for the canon target; acceptable for a pilot. E2 should aim for 10–11k.
