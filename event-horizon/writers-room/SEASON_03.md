# Season 3: APERTURE (SEALED)

Sophomore fall at Temple, 10 episodes, **Aug 22 – Dec 2027**. The look is Bronze Age (`look: 's3'`): warmer paper, browner inks, muted CMYK.

**Season engine:** Halcyon stops hiding. On the one-year anniversary, Achterberg goes public: the **Broad Street Exposure Registry**, a "public-health response" to a "neurological exposure event" on Aug 22, 2026. It comes with free care, a hotline and a city partnership. The hunt becomes a clinic. Recovery now arrives with a city health badge, in daylight.
- **Catherine Pemberton** is the Registry's medical advisor (paid by Halcyon). She is complicit, and becomes horrified once she learns her daughter is ES-44.
- **Hugh** works the Registry's outreach. He is the friendly face.
- **Bruno** is at war with Halcyon. His Changed have gone to ground.
- Sam is **Second Hand** in the papers (this save).
- The Registry's real purpose is building toward the **SECOND DRAW**: reopening the door. Achterberg needs a Pemberton to hold it.

**One-line arc:** a public city learns there's something to be afraid of, and a private couple learns what they're willing to carry for each other.

**Romance (written to this save):** the bible's "get together by S3's end" is already met: they are together and said "I love you" in S2E10. So S3 *tests* it. The tests:
- Jules's luck debt.
- Catherine.
- Hugh.
- The temptation to decide for each other.
- The Revenant's interest in Jules.

Player choices can strain it or deepen it. The finale needs them standing in the door *together*.
