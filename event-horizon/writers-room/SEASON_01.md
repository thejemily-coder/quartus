# Season 1: STREET LEVEL (SEALED)

Freshman fall at Temple, 10 episodes, from move-in (Thu Aug 20) to winter break. The look is Silver Age.

**Season engine:** Sam gets powers and does what Sam does, which is run toward things. The season's big bad is a mirror (Teddy Kowal's crew). The season's real antagonist is Sam's own savior reflex. Jules is the person he can't impress and then can't stop thinking about. Behind everything, Halcyon is quietly counting the Changed.

**Season's one-line arc:** a kid learns that being fast isn't the same as being in time.

## Pilot beat sheet (Episode 1, premiere-length)

- **Cold open (Sat 11:51 PM):** Broad & Spring Garden. A SEPTA bus, the light. The world stops. *"Four days earlier."* Origin choice in a memory beat.
- **Main titles.**
- **Act One, "Move-In" (Thu):** Frank's cab up Broad Street, the family, Gigi, Nonna's warning ("Wear a jacket Saturday"). Cardone Hall. Nik. **The minifridge:** Sam carries Jules's minifridge up 9 flights during the elevator queue without being asked. Her driver had it. It goes badly. Grace the RA. Simone.
- **Act Two, "Welcome Week" (Fri):** a hub with 3 slots: floor icebreaker, Ciro's job, Vinnie on FaceTime, a frat party on Diamond St, the library, calling home. **Pool table** at the student center (fixed scene): Jules runs the table, Sam bets $40 he doesn't have, and loses. Dislike locked in, though with one flicker.
- **Act Three, "The Hitch" (Sat):** a delivery shift, then the order to Broad & Spring Garden. 11:51:04. The world stops. Sam moves. The bus with the kid (Jayden, 8). A **forced Rewind** on failure: the first diegetic moment. Jules is in the crowd at the Welcome Week concert on Liacouras Walk. (Sam sees her later in the chaos. The player sees a die turn gold.)
- **Act Four, "Wawa Guy" (Sun–Mon):** aftermath, sleepless. Testing powers (with or without Nik, the first big knowledge choice). Mikey calls. The phone thief at the Wawa. A video goes viral. First day of classes: Philadelphia Neighborhoods with Dr. Weisz, who looks at Sam too long. Jules sits next to him in the class (by luck).
- **Tag (Mon night):** Jules alone in her room rolling a die, sixes twenty times in a row. *(This is non-interactive and not Sam's POV, framed as "the camera.")* Then Sam on Broad in the rain after the Wawa, and the Revenant across the street, finger to lips. A bus passes, and he's gone.
