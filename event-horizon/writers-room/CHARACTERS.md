# Voice Sheets (SEALED)

Use these for voice QA. Each entry covers how the character talks, what they want, what they hide, what they never do, and how they change.

Engine speaker IDs are given in brackets, e.g. `[sam]`. Dialogue in episode markup is written as `ID: line`.

---

## Sam Kearney [sam]
- **Talks:** fast, joking, run-on when nervous. South Philly: "youse," "down the shore," "wooder" (only when he's tired or home), "jawn" (sparingly, he knows it's corny), "hoagie" never "sub," "gravy" never "sauce" (Sunday gravy), "the El," "the Linc," "Iggles" when mocking his dad. Calls his mother "Ma," his grandmother "Nonna," and his dad "Pop." Swears casually, and never in front of Nonna.
- **Wants:** to matter. To be the one who shows up.
- **Hides:** that he thinks he's the family's afterthought: Mikey's the hero, Gigi's the baby, Sam's the one who got in fights. That he cried in his car at his high school graduation.
- **Never:** abandons someone in front of him, no matter the cost. That's his virtue and his flaw. He is never cruel when he's calm, though he can be cruel when he's angry and then hates himself for it.
- **Changes:** his jokes get shorter as the seasons go on. By Season 6 he's quiet more often than not. Write his growth unevenly: he relapses into the savior reflex under stress.
- **Player shaping:** his "high school self" origin choice in the pilot sets one attribute and a flag (`origin_*`). Honor it in later callbacks: shortstop at Neumann-Goretti, the line at Dolores' Luncheonette, the talker, the reader, the fighter, Nonna's caretaker.

## Jules Pemberton [jules]
- **Talks:** precise, dry, full sentences, devastating economy. Her vocabulary is a weapon. She almost never swears, so when she does, it lands. Says "Mother" to Catherine's face and "my mother" about her. "Gran." Never uses Philly slang. By Season 2 she has picked up "jawn" from Sam, ironically, then not ironically.
- **Wants:** to be chosen for herself, not for her name, her face, or her competence. To lose control safely once.
- **Hides:** the poker game, the power (Season 1), that she's never been in love (she tells Callum she loves him because it's expected), that she sleeps with the light on.
- **Never:** begs. Cries in front of people (until she does, and it's an event).
- **With Sam, Season 1:** irritation, then grudging amusement (Episode 2), then *the night* (Episode 3, target), after which she's undone and furious about it. Her teasing of Sam is how she flirts, and she doesn't realize she's doing it.
- **Changes:** loosens. Laughs with her whole body by Season 3. Her later-season power (Fate) makes her *more* frightened of control, not less.

## Nik Bhattacharya [nik]
- **Talks:** long anxious paragraphs with self-interruptions, pop-culture references, gamer vocabulary ("that's a skill issue," "patch notes"), Bengali endearments for his mom on the phone ("Ma, haan, haan, I'm eating"). Calls Sam "bro" unironically and "Salvatore" when he's scared.
- **Wants:** to be in the story, not watching it. To be braver than his body.
- **Hides:** panic attacks. That he's applying to transfer to Rutgers to be closer to home (Season 1), and that he withdraws the application because of Sam.
- **Never:** quits on a friend. Lies well (he's terrible at it).
- **Function:** the guy in the chair, comic relief, the moral center in miniature.

## Simone Okonkwo-Hayes [simone]
- **Talks:** big, theatrical, quotable. Calls people by full names to destabilize them ("Salvatore Kearney, sit DOWN"). Southern warmth plus theater-kid drama. Her perception is surgical: she says the true thing everyone's avoiding, then makes a joke so you can survive it.
- **Wants:** to be seen as more than the funny one. To be the lead in her own life.
- **Hides:** an ex in Atlanta who outed her. That she notices *everything*, including Jules's luck: she keeps a list on her phone titled "Things That Don't Add Up About Juliet."
- **Never:** lets a lie slide forever. She gives you exactly one chance to tell her yourself.
- **Romance:** queer. Possible arcs with Grace (Season 2+) or a new character. Write to the save.

## Vinnie Russo [vinnie]
- **Talks:** blunt, loud, funny, crude, deep South Philly. "Jawn" freely, "yo" as punctuation, "bol" (old-head) when he's being affectionate. Calls Sam "Sally" (only Vinnie can) or "college."
- **Wants:** respect. To not be left behind. To provide for his mother and younger brothers (his father is in prison at Curran-Fromhold).
- **Hides:** that he applied to Temple too and was waitlisted, and never told Sam.
- **Never:** rats. That's the code that will damn him or save him.

## Grace Lim [grace]
- **Talks:** dry, clinical, under-slept. Medical precision in casual moments ("That's not a bruise, that's a hematoma, and you're an idiot"). Says "okay" as a full sentence.
- **Wants:** to finish. MCAT, med school, a life. And, secretly, for something to *happen* to her.
- **Hides:** that she's on academic probation for a semester she doesn't talk about (her father's stroke).

## Rosa Kearney [rosa]
- Fierce, warm, Italian-American mother. Sees through everything. "Salvatore." Feeds people as an act of war. Pragmatic about violence ("I'm a school nurse, I've seen worse on a playground"). DiNardo time-sense: her uncanny timing ("I knew you'd call").

## Frank Kearney [frank]
- Few words. Irish-Catholic, cab driver, Eagles. Expresses love through car maintenance and driving. "Kid." His shame is the debt. He's a better man than he thinks and a worse liar than he hopes.

## Nonna Teresa DiNardo [nonna]
- 81. Italian-English mix ("Madonna mia," "mangia," "stunad," "che cazzo"). Runs the block. Says uncanny things as if they're ordinary: "The clocks will stop soon. Wear a jacket." Knows about 1918. Loves Sam ferociously and calls him "Salvatò."

## Mikey Kearney [mikey]
- 26, PPD patrol in the 3rd District (South Philly). Big brother, ball-buster, black-and-white morality, genuinely good cop in a hard system. Calls Sam "Sammy." Investigates the Blur from Episode 2 on.

## Gigi Kearney [gigi]
- 13. Communicates in texts (all lowercase, emoji, "SAMMMMM"). Fearless, funny, nosy. Idolizes Sam. **Never written in any sexual context.**

## Dr. Catherine Pemberton [catherine]
- Cold precision, perfect diction, love expressed as standards. "Juliet." Calls Temple "that school." Her love for Jules is real and expressed entirely wrong.

## Whitney Pemberton [whitney]
- Warm, absent, effortlessly charming. Calls Jules "Jules-bug." Always on a call. His charm is the scariest thing about him once you know.

## Hugh Pemberton [hugh]
- Golden, glib, competitive, secretly insecure. Calls Jules "Jubilee" (an old twin joke). Wants what she has, whatever it is.

## Constance Pemberton [constance]
- 84. Razor wit. Says exactly what she means in the fewest possible words. Smokes one cigarette a day at 4 pm. Calls Jules "child." Knows everything about the bargain and lies about none of it if asked the right question.

## Callum Whitford [callum]
- Earnest, polite, athletic, kind, a little dull. "Jules" with a hopeful lilt. He'll be a good husband to somebody. Don't make him a villain.

## Teddy Kowal [teddy]
- Port Richmond, Polish, soft-spoken, smart, tired. "Listen." Avoids violence and is brutal when cornered. Loves his mother and his cousin. Dark mirror of Sam.

## Bree Kowal [bree]
- 18, funny, sardonic, plays back other people's words in their voices as jokes (her power makes this literal). Wants out.

## Heck Ramos [heck]
- Gentle giant, former boxer, Spanish-English, says "papa" to younger guys. When he burns, he burns.

## Femi Ojo [femi]
- Temple junior, econ major, wry, lonely, invisible in every sense. He liked being unnoticed until it started to mean something. Dies in Episode 8.

## Ciro Bianchi [ciro]
- 60s, Neapolitan. Speaks only in insults ("You deliver like a funeral"). Loves Sam. Pays cash.

## Dana Fiorillo [dana]
- Inquirer metro reporter, 30s, sharp, ambitious, fair. Names the heroes. Later, a possible ally.

## The Revenant [old_sam]
- Rarely speaks. When he does, his voice is Sam's voice worn down to the wire. His phrasing is Sam's jokes with the jokes removed. Smells like ozone and wet pennies. Missing his left ring finger.
